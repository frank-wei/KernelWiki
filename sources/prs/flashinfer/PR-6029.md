## Summary

Adds the FP4 1024- and 4096-token model routes (384 experts, top-6, hidden 5120, intermediate 2304, activation clamp 10) to the generated `mega_moe_v3` pipeline family for `sm_100a` and `sm_103a`, so the v3 family covers every model route the `source_mega_moe` family serves. Nothing else in the catalog changes: the retained v3 programs are byte-identical, the schema is unchanged and `source_mega_moe` is untouched.

Refs #5842 #5843

## What changed

- `flashinfer/experimental/mega_moe_v3/`: two new generated programs per architecture (kernel + binding) and their catalog routes; the host preparation now sizes the source-block workspace with the per-route expert tile height (16 rows up to 512 tokens, 32 rows at 1024, 128 rows at 4096) and applies the per-block readiness form to the two long routes, exactly as the generator's launcher does.
- `tests/experimental/test_generated_mega_moe.py`: `test_v3_long_token_routes[1024|4096]` (reference check, replay, single-kernel graph, workspace lifecycle).
- `examples/experimental/mega_moe_inputs.py`, `docs/experimental/generated_mega_moe.md`: route lists.
