## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, this PR re-exports the Cake-generated MiniMax Sparse Attention family for SM100 / SM103 with the CAKE-1183 levers accepted in rounds 9-10 that are not yet on `main`.
> Two route groups change: uniform-FP8 decode (D-U1, D-U2, D-U2b) and Top-K select (S2, tradix v2, per-architecture `topk_select`). Prefill routes do not change. This is one commit directly on `main` `fb83b0ee` and is not stacked.
> No lever changes the arithmetic. Outputs and Top-K indices are bitwise identical in every Cake A/B.
> Speedup vs the reference kernels (vllm-project/MSA `dev` @ `968061ab`, `fmha_sm100`), same job, geomean (min-max):
>
> | rows | GB300 (sm_103a) | B200 (sm_100a) |
> |---|---|---|
> | FP8 decode, 14 rows (12 uniform-FP8 q8 + 2 legacy q1) | **1.747×** (1.511-4.473) | **1.619×** (1.374-4.586) |
> | uniform-FP8 q8 decode alone, 12 rows | 1.511-1.554× | 1.374-1.423× |
> | Top-K, 9 of 10 rows (with a reference route) | **2.404×** (1.296-5.257) | **2.358×** (1.323-5.493) |
