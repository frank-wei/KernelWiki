## 📌 Description

Add `sm90_fp8_mxfp4_bf16_pull_cutedsl` for fused Hopper MegaMoE with FP8 activations, packed MXFP4 weights, and BF16 output. The backend accepts raw E2M1 payloads and K32 E8M0 scales, applies Humming preprocessing, and keeps weight-format, workspace, compilation, and tuning-cache identities distinct from FP8.

Online `knobs="auto"` and `flashinfer.moe_ep.tune --dtype sm90_mxfp4` share the bounded `hopper_mxfp4_candidates()` list. The list includes guarded FC2 N8 tails, segmented FC1 readiness, and `(1,2,1)` tail-pair scheduling. Tail candidates are filtered by each tactic's shape and protocol requirements, with no exact-model whitelist; H7168/I3072/E384/EP4 retains the same 38 candidates. Complete explicit tactics can be replayed without tuning. Ordinary CUDA Graph replay, repeated workspaces, empty experts/ranks, and small-value numerical behavior have dedicated tests.
