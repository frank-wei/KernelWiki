## 📌 Description

`minimax_h3_bf16_pre_attention` (SM100a / SM103a) becomes a two-launch stage: a norm + indexed-AdaLN kernel writes the modulated BF16 activation into a caller-owned `[M, 5376]` workspace, and a persistent 2-CTA `tcgen05` GEMM streams the workspace and the fused QKV weight through TMA with the per-head Q/K RMSNorm, partial NeoX RoPE and destination-major pack fused into its epilogue.

Why: the previous fused single kernel materialized the activation tile inside the GEMM CTA and was latency-bound at `ulysses_degree = 1` (17.2 ms at M = 32640 on B300 against 4.9 ms for the segmented RMSNorm + cuBLAS + fused QK-norm/RoPE chain). The weight is consumed in the engine-resident `[qkv_kind, head, head_dim]` row order (`[q_all | k_all | v_all]`), as introduced in the previous commit of this branch.

API: `minimax_h3_bf16_pre_attention(..., workspace=None)` — optional contiguous BF16 `[M, 5376]` scratch (allocated per call when omitted; CUDA-graph users may pass a persistent buffer). The `P = 1` segmented-fallback advice in the docstring is withdrawn.
