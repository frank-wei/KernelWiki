## Summary

Re-renders the two Cake-exported Kimi-K3 MXFP4 SiTU MoE backends from Cake revision `d28a547564d7` with one command. The backends are `flashinfer/fused_moe/cake/` for `backend="cake"` (cuda_cpp) and `flashinfer/fused_moe/cake_cute/` for `backend="cake_cute"` (CuTe DSL). Their device sources live under `csrc/fused_moe/cake_mxfp4_situ_moe/{cuda,cute}/sm_103a`. The manifests pin `producer_revision` `d28a547564d7bab2f8d5a489414db7ba1b6b63a5`. The previous pin was `2b16984333b551b1a426c0aaff9d88907836adc0`, the export of #6452, and the new producer revision contains its content. This PR is export + evidence only; no FlashInfer API change.

What the re-render brings (one Cake change set lowered to both backends):

- **Swap-AB GEMM1 cluster split-K twins (`_cs`)**: a two-CTA (1,1,2) cluster splits K between the two CTAs. The peer CTA stores its alpha-scaled 128 x n fp32 accumulator to the leader's shared memory (`st.async`). The leader adds the two fp32 partials and runs the SiTU epilogue, in the same association order as the hand-written cluster_split path. The plan selects:
  - the n8 kbps-8 twin on the T ≤ 16 rows of both layouts
  - the n32 twin on the expert-parallel rows
