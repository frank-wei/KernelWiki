## What changed

Host-only consolidation of the single-file SM120 (RTX 5090 / RTX PRO 6000) MiniMax-H3 operators, regenerated by the
Cake exporters. No kernel body changes: every kernel compiled for `sm_120a` is SASS-identical to the base tree (see
evidence).

- **Two shared headers under `csrc/`.** `cake_minimax_h3_sm120_host.cuh` carries the tensor checks, tensor-map encoders,
  per-module device-configuration cache and the persistent launch plan that the pre-attention, FC1+SwiGLU, out-proj and
  FP8 varlen-attention translation units each re-implemented; `cake_minimax_h3_sm120_norm_adaln_quant.cuh` carries the two
  RMSNorm + AdaLN + activation-quantization kernels that the pre-attention and FC1 files compiled from byte-identical private
  copies. Each translation unit keeps a few-line wrapper (its anchor tensor name, row bytes / swizzle, its own dynamic
  shared-memory
