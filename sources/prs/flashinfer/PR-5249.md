## 📌 Description

Add experimental cuDNN Frost MoE backends for BF16 × BF16, MXFP8 × MXFP8, NVFP4 × NVFP4, and MXFP8 × MXFP4, supporting ten gated and non-gated activations.

The integration ships standalone Frost-generated CuTe DSL kernels with shared JIT compilation and caching. Geometry configurations are injected into source templates at runtime and remain compile-time constants, reducing duplicated kernel sources. Each supported problem profile registers two fused FC1 + activation configurations × two FC2 configurations, giving MoELayer four Frost plans to autotune alongside existing backends.

End-to-end benchmarks on NVIDIA Rubin (SM107), at a target SM clock of 1100 MHz, cover 800 cases per dtype. Results compare the complete eligible existing backend pool against the same pool with Frost added.

   Activation × weight dtype, Geomean speedup, Peak speedup
   BF16 × BF16 | 1.081× | 1.594×
   MXFP8 × MXFP8 | 1.155× | 1.627×
   NVFP4 × NVFP4 | 1.088× | 1.576×
   MXFP8 × MXFP4 | 1.137× | 1.645×
