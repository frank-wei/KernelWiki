## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
This PR adds `sm103_mxfp8_mxfp8_bf16_cake`, an experimental Cake backend for FlashInfer's `moe_ep` MegaMoE EP16 kernel family (the `MoEEpMegaLayer` API). It uses MXFP8 E4M3 activations and MXFP8 E4M3 weights with E8M0 block-32 scales, produces BF16 output, and runs EP16 over NVSHMEM symmetric memory with hidden 3072, intermediate 5120, 512 experts (32 per rank), top-8, and at most 64 tokens per rank. The existing variants for this MXFP8 x MXFP8 -> BF16 configuration are the CuTe-DSL `sm100_mxfp8_mxfp8_bf16_cutedsl` backend in its legacy inference form and its GenPhase form (the `knobs=None` heuristic and the `knobs="auto"` autotune), which was introduced by #6611 and merged as #6612. Compared with the GenPhase backend, the Cake backend is 1.07x-1.11x faster than the `knobs=None` heuristic and 1.05x-1.10x faster than `knobs="auto"` across the six shapes below, and 1.16x-1.18x faster than the legacy inference form.

| Shape | Cake backend (this PR), us | GenPhase heuristic (knobs=None), us | Speedup vs heuristic | GenPhase auto (knobs="auto"), us | Speedup vs auto |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| T=256 global tokens (16 per rank), balanced routing | 264.4 | 292.2 | 1.105x | 291.3 |
