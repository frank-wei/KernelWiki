## Summary

Cake all-gather matmul (`flashinfer.comm.all_gather_matmul`, Blackwell SM100 / SM103, TP2/TP4/TP8): the device
programs and the host sequence of every route change so that the NVLink gather and the tcgen05 GEMM overlap
instead of running back to back. Numerics are unchanged: same operands, same dtype, fp32 accumulation over the
full K, and every delivered row's output is bitwise identical to the previous delivery's.

* **Arrival-ordered persistent main kernel.** One CTA per SM strides over the output tiles in the order their
  rows land (local shard first, then each peer's chunks as its readiness epoch arrives). The static peer-partition
  grid, whose CTAs spun on a signal while holding their SM, is replaced on every route. Large-M rows gain up to
  1.6x; the Llama TP4 rows 1.1–1.4x.
* **SM push kernel for small shards.** Calls with at most 512 padded local rows at TP4/TP8 whose N is within the
  route's width ceiling (`sm_push_max_cols`: 10240 at 128 padded rows, 4096 at 512) copy the local shard into every
  peer's scratch with one kernel of system-scope 16-byte stores (one launch for all peers, one readiness epoch per
  chunk) instead of the per-peer copy-engine loop
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
