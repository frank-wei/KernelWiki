## Summary

Follow-up to the DeepSeek-V3.2 DSA indexer forwarding (#6059): one kernel change and one admission-policy change in the `deepgemm_dense_mqa` family.

1. **64-head FP8 MQA logits program:** the per-row weighted ReLU sum now lowers through the folded abs-in-PTX form with two accumulator chains, which lets the compiler interleave the 64 add/fma pairs per row with the TMEM loads and the store-address math instead of the pinned `asm volatile` pair order. Output is bitwise identical. Per KV block the program is 2-3 % faster on both architectures (GB300 slope 0.955 → 0.974, B200 0.927 → 0.949 relative to the DeepGEMM reference); the admitted long-prefix rows gain 1-2 %. The admitted tiers are unchanged: `fp8:h64:partial:any` on sm_103a, nothing on sm_100a (the prefix-0 rows of the smaller tiers still sit at or below parity and keep stock DeepGEMM).
