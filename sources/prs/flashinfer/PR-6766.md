## Summary

This PR does steps 1-4 of #6654 for the Cake-generated Blackwell MSA family. It covers the dense programs in `csrc/blackwell_msa/` (registry and loader in `flashinfer/jit/blackwell_msa.py`, dispatcher in `flashinfer/msa_ops/_blackwell_sm100.py`) and the JIT-only NVFP4 sparse-decode backend in `flashinfer/experimental/msa_nvfp4_decode/`:
- Removes dead registry fields, route aliases, flags and stale docs.
- Switches the NVFP4 backend from its own copies of the device helpers and host preamble to the shared ones.
- Merges the per-arch uniform-FP8 decode pair into one program.
- Defines the nine argument plans once.

For every input, program selection, launch arguments and results are unchanged.
