## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, this PR regenerates the **prefill** routes of the Cake-generated MiniMax Sparse Attention family for
> SM100 / SM103 with every prefill lever the CAKE-1183 ceiling round accepted since the family's last prefill delivery (#6397): the paged
> `first_topk` rows run **1.8–2.7× faster** (2.0–2.7× on 8 of the 10 rows; B200 128K 1.76×, GB300 64K 1.94×; e.g. paged b1 q8192: 965 → 411 µs on B200, 885 → 332 µs on GB300; round-9 merged-head
> acceptance, medians of the two interleaved passes), the flat GQA-16 q4096 row **1.18× / 1.11×**, the long flat rows 1.01–1.04× on the round-9 rows (the q8192 row PARITY) and
> 1.02–1.08× on the WIN rows of the rounds-4–8 head (the remaining long flat rows PARITY), with **0 LOSS** on the 10 round-9 rows per architecture
> and on the 18 (GB300) / 17 measured (B200, 1 GAP: baseline arm OOM-infeasible) rounds-4–8 rows. Every lever is a schedule, pipeline-depth, work-list, tile-order, host-plan or
> host-launch change; outputs are bitwise identical to the replaced programs except on the four paged 16K–128K `first_topk` rows that move
> onto the selection-union route (closer to the FP32 reference than before, see "Precision").
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
