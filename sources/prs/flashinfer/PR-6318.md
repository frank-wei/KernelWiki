## Summary

Re-export of the `deepgemm_dense_mqa` family (dense 32-head and 64-head FP8 / FP4 MQA logits, paged FP8 MQA logits) from the producer tip of the convergence round against the stock DeepGEMM kernels (`fp8_mqa_logits` / `fp8_paged_mqa_logits`, sgl-deep-gemm 0.2.0). Catalog schema `dense_mqa.v7` -> `dense_mqa.v8`.

### `deepgemm_dense_mqa`

1. **64-head FP8 dense logits -- production text chosen per architecture.** On SM100a the accumulator is a two-stage TMEM ring (the next row's MMA fills the second stage while the math roles drain the first); on SM103a the MMA and math roles wait on the producer's source-side barriers. The one-split short-KV text is shared. The catalog therefore binds a stage's program per architecture (`stages = [[stage, {arch: program}], ...]`); `dense_mqa.stage_program(stage, arch)` / `program_names(route, arch=...)` resolve the device's program, the catalog checker requires every delivered (program, architecture) pair to be routed, and only the device's programs are built. Routing, admission (`dense_admission`: every 64-head route admitted on both architectures) and the `:short` rule are unchanged.
