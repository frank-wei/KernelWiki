## Summary

> **Speedup over DeepGEMM (geomean of the 216 shapes per device, kernel time, measured with `max_context_hint`): RTX 5090 1.23×, RTX PRO 6000 1.20×.** Ratios below are Cake / DeepGEMM time (0.8151 and 0.8354); r2 without the hint measured 1.21× / 1.19×. Best shapes reach 2.5× (32-head `next_n` 5 at batch 256); 199 of 216 shapes are faster on the RTX 5090 and 203 of 216 on the RTX PRO 6000; the slower shapes (12 / 10) are DeepGEMM-identical schedules within +2.2 %.

This PR updates the SM120a paged FP8 MQA logits package, the DeepSeek-V3.2 / V4.1-Flash decode lightning indexer. It replaces all of the generated programs from #6180, #6199 and #6219.

A larger Q atom scores more tokens in each pass over the KV cache. Small batches still need DeepGEMM's 2-token atoms to balance work across SMs, unless their contexts are long. So each call now picks its atom.

- **Batch-aware Q-atom variants.** Each call picks a Q-atom program from the head count, `next_n` and batch class.
- **`num_heads` keyword.** `get_paged_mqa_logits_metadata` gains a keyword-only `num_heads`. It is needed only for `next_n` 5/6 at batch > 8, and at batch 2..8 when `max_context_hint` is at least 16384.
