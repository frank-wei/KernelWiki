## 📌 Description

Adds native SM120/SM121 NVFP4 W4A4 kernels to `mm_fp4(..., backend="cute-dsl")`. The kernels consume canonical packed weights and physical 128x4 E4M3 scales directly, without repacking or a second weight copy. Sharing these buffers with W4A16 in #5242 enables dynamic W4A4/W4A16 selection.

Independent staged kernels handle small and ragged shapes; persistent ping-pong kernels handle aligned larger shapes. Support includes positive M, N/K divisible by 64, BF16 output, live GPU alpha and CUDA graph replay.

**Performance on DGX Spark (SM121):** the 27B down projection (`N=5120, K=17408`) is **1.69x faster at M=4096 and 2.13x at M=8192 than default CUTLASS**, or **1.17x and 1.41x with both backends autotuned**. Geomeans across the measured shapes:

| Shapes | Count | Default speedup | Autotuned speedup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Official, M <= 16 | 78 | 1.208x | 1.071x |
| Official, M = 64 | 32 | 1.078x | 1.052x |
| Official, M >= 128 | 49 | 0.970x | 0.942x |
| All official shapes | 159 | 1.103x | 1.026x |
| 27B, M = 4096/8192 | 4 | 1.408x | 1.126x |
