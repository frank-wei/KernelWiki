## 📌 Description

Extends the cuDNN Frost MoE backend on SM120 (`sm_120a`, consumer/workstation Blackwell) from BF16 normal-orientation kernels to all four formats with swap-AB. It follows the SM107 block-scale backends (#5249, #5628, #5891, #6035) and the SM120 BF16 port (#5762):

- **New:** SM120 MXFP8, NVFP4 and MXFP8 × MXFP4 grouped-GEMM kernels from cuDNN Frost's `sm120_moe_grouped_block_scale_matmul_fwd[_swap_ab]` templates, in normal and swap-AB orientations, for all ten FC1 activations and FC2. MXFP8 and MXFP8 × MXFP4 FC1 also ship fused intermediate quantization (normal orientation, STG).
- **BF16:** adds the swap-AB template (`sm120_moe_grouped_matmul_fwd_swap_ab`) and re-selects the SM120 pool.
- All SM120 sources are re-exported from cuDNN Frontend `89f40871` (previously `a74b1b21`); cuDNN Frontend itself is not modified. SM107 records and sources are unchanged.

`CudnnFrost{Mxfp8,Nvfp4,Mxfp8Mxfp4}Config.supported(120)` now returns `True`. On SM120 these candidates join `MoELayer` auto-selection as they do on SM107, with no experimental opt-in.
