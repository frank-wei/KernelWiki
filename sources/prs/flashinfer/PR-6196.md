## 📌 Description

Performance follow-up to #6169 (MiniMax-H3 SM120 fused quantized MLP, RTX 5090 / RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell), two generated-source commits:

1. **FP8 FC1 raster group 64.** The two FP8 FC1 + SwiGLU kernels (`fc1_fp8`, legacy e4m3 form for the RTX PRO 6000; `fc1_fp8_mx`, `kind::mxf8f6f4` form for the RTX 5090) specialize the persistent raster with `GROUP_M 64` instead of 16: 64 M tiles share each 128-wide N tile of the prepacked FC1 weight before the raster advances, so the A operand of one raster group (44 MB) stays L2-resident on both boards while the 154 MB weight streams once per group (5 passes at M = 38592 instead of 19). NVFP4 keeps group 16 (half the A bytes; group 32 measured slower on the RTX PRO 6000 and within the noise band on the RTX 5090).
