## Summary

This PR optimizes the kernels of the Cake MoK MegaMoE training step in `flashinfer/experimental/cake_mok_bf16` (#6053). It covers forward, recompute and backward, with BF16 and MXFP8 routed experts, on SM100a and SM103a. Precision, correctness and the recipe do not change.
