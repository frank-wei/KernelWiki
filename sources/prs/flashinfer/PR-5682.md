## Summary

`Sm100BlockScaledContiguousGroupedGemmFinalizeFusionKernel` reads `num_non_exiting_tiles` and the per-tile group lists before its `griddepcontrol.wait`: the wait sat after the cluster barrier, after the persistent tile scheduler had already produced its first tile. Under programmatic dependent launch the finalize kernel may start while the routing kernel two launches back is still writing those buffers, because the intermediate dense GEMM1 triggers its dependents at kernel entry, before its own wait. On a stale group index the scheduler warp faulted. In a graph-replay loop this reproduced about once in 30 replays.
