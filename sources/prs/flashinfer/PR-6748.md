## Summary

- **Sources:** at base, `csrc/cake_msa_icp_indexer/` holds 54 files: 12 scorer kernels, 14 exchange-merge kernels, 26 bindings and 2 headers.
- **Registry and loader:** `flashinfer/experimental/cake_msa_icp_indexer/cake_jit.py` (`PROGRAMS`, `programs_for` / `select_*`, `gen_program_spec`) defines 52 JIT modules: 26 records x 2 targets (sm100a, sm103a).
