## 📌 Description

Regenerates the SM100/SM103 CUDA sources of the MiniMax-H3 fused RMSNorm + AdaLN + FC1 + SwiGLU operator (#6039) and of the fused MLP operator that shares its norm/AdaLN kernels, from the upstream generator at `83264f1c122`:

- `csrc/cake_minimax_h3_fc1_swiglu_sm100a.cu`, `csrc/cake_minimax_h3_fc1_swiglu_sm103a.cu`
- `csrc/cake_minimax_h3_mlp_sm100a.cu`, `csrc/cake_minimax_h3_mlp_sm103a.cu`

**What changed in the kernels.** Functionally, only the norm/AdaLN(+quantize) prologue kernels: the BF16 round points use the packed `__float22bfloat162_rn` intrinsic (one `cvt.rn.bf16x2.f32`) each, the RMSNorm sum of squares is accumulated with `fma.rn.f32.bf16` directly from the packed BF16 words (same sequential FP32 FMA chain), and `BF16(scale + 1)` is one `add.rn.bf16x2`. Instruction-form only: every output is **bitwise identical** to the previous generated kernels on the full validation grid of all three variants (BF16 / MXFP8 / NVFP4) on both architectures (verified with `torch.equal`, 56 rows per variant per architecture per round, two rounds, incl. M = 1, unaligned tails and out-of-range AdaLN indices). The GEMM kernels are functionally unchanged: the MLP GEMM kernels
