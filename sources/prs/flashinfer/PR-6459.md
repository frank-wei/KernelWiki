## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, this PR regenerates the Top-K select routes of the Cake-generated MiniMax Sparse Attention family for
> SM100 / SM103 with three Cake levers: (1) the one-CTA-per-row **streaming Top-K16 program** dumps and reads its candidates rank-major, so
> the 24 % of its cycles that went to shared-memory bank conflicts vanish (lever C2-S); (2) every argmax round of that program keeps **one CTA
> barrier instead of two** (lever C1-S); (3) the **host plan** sends 16-query grids of at most 32 (query, head) rows (at most 27 head × query-tile groups, so the
> `S = 16` grid fits one wave) over 513..1024 valid pages to the stage-only `S = 16` split of the existing `topk_select` program instead of the streaming program (lever T11b-2, host rule
> only). On the frozen Top-K rows the streaming t64 rows run **1.395 / 1.395× (B200) and 1.401 / 1.408× (GB300)** faster and the small-grid
> t8 rows **1.11 / 1.11× (B200) and 1.13 / 1.12× (GB300)** (1.11 / 1.10× and 1.12 / 1.12× against the plan of #6410), every other row PARITY, 0 LOSS, with **bit-identical selected indices**. Based on
> `main` `e403d307` (#6443, #6397 and #6410 are merged).
