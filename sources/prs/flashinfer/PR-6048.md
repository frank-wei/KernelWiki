## Summary

Regenerates the Cake contiguous grouped FP8 GEMM family (`cake_grouped_fp8_gemm`) and adds a **block-scaled UE8M0 family** for the compact MoE layout that sglang's `moe_fp8_grouped` route (DeepGEMM contiguous mode) produces on Qwen3.5.
