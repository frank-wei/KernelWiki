## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, this PR does two things to the Cake-generated MiniMax Sparse Attention decode routes for SM100 / SM103:
> (1) it **fixes an intermittent hang** in the uniform-FP8 and BF16-Q / FP8-KV (`xform2`) decode programs — a barrier-phase race that is
> present in the programs on `main` and in #6397, reproduced 15 times on 5 GB300 nodes, and fixed by barrier bookkeeping only (outputs
> bitwise identical); and (2) it regenerates the six **M16 16-bit decode programs** without four synchronisation sites that order no
> dependency — **never slower** on the 24 measured pure-M16 rows per architecture (B200: 9 rows WIN at 1.020–1.028×, 15 PARITY, 0 LOSS;
> GB300: 1 WIN at 1.025×, 23 PARITY, 0 LOSS) with **bitwise-identical outputs**. Every decode route of the family is re-pointed; the Top-K
> and prefill routes are unchanged. Originally stacked on the Top-K PR #6410 and #6397; both merged on 2026-10-14, and this PR was rebased onto `main` `13149016` as a single commit (`e3122c1f`, delta unchanged).
