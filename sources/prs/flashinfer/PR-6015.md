## Kimi-K3 NVFP4 SiTU experts: SM100 + SM103 claim-8 FC2 at 2 CTAs/SM

Follow-up to the claim-8 FC2 direct-store epilogue: the eight tile-N16 claim-8 rows (M = 32, 64, 128, 256; uniform and skewed routing) on SM103 (B300) and on SM100 (B200) move to an FC2 program variant that runs two CTAs per SM with six live work slots, and its device work-feed pool widens from 6 to 12 rows (336 FC2 CTAs on 148 SMs) on both architectures. Two commits, one per architecture: the first routes the SM103 rows (two new program records, one route flip, SM103 pool branch), the second routes the SM100 rows (two new program records, two route flips, pool branch widened to both architectures).

**Files**: `flashinfer/fused_moe/cake_kimi_k3_situ.py` (pool branch of the FC2 work-feed geometry), `flashinfer/jit/cake_kimi_k3_situ.py` (four new program records, three route flips), three generated translation units under `csrc/fused_moe/cake_kimi_k3_situ/sm_103a/` and three under `csrc/fused_moe/cake_kimi_k3_situ/sm_100a/`. No API, tutorial or test change; workspace size is unchanged.
