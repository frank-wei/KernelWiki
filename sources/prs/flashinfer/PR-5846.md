## What

Every exported Blackwell KDA prefill body now takes the q / k / v token pitch from its TensorView (TMA descriptors and the raw tail loads), so the strided `split(dim=-1)` views of one packed `[T, 3·H·128]` projection row that serving hands over are launched in place on every route. Previously only the fused M128 body read that pitch; the other bodies (M64 value split, BT16 prepare/chain, persistent M128, small-BH, TF32 fused / fused N32) declared dense pitches and `FlashKDABlackwellBF16FusedLaunch` densified q / k / v into launch-owned staging copies (three `T·H·128` BF16 copies per call, refreshed on every launch since #5738).

Bodies that reach an operand only through its TMA descriptor (BT16 prepare: q, k; BT16 chain and small-BH: v; TF32 fused N32: q, k, v; TF32 fused: q, k) receive a one-element carrier for the raw pointer of the same name, the way `beta` already does, because the generated FFI shim keeps the contiguous host contract on a raw pointer without pitc
