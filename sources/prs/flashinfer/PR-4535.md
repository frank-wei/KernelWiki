## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Developed by the **CAKE team**, the kernels in this PR **outperform** the relevant **SOTA baselines at the time of merge** on the **vast majority of performance benchmark shapes**, matching them otherwise.

Tracks #4254.

## Summary

- Treat `lower_bound=None` as Kimi-Linear's native unbounded-softplus gate, `-exp(A_log) * softplus(g + dt_bias)`.
- Route the BF16/D128 equal-Q/KV-head contract at local H4/H8/H16/H32 (TP8/TP4/TP2/TP1) to dedicated frozen `cake_kda` kernels on SM100a and SM103a.
- Accept packed Q/K/V plus independently strided gate and beta views without staging copies, while preserving indexed/noncompact state pools, final state, and optional packed intermediate checkpoints.
- Use the checkpoint-aligned BT64 prefill recurrence for unbounded local-H4 serving with nonzero 64-aligned checkpoints; general H4, checkpoint-32, H8, H16, H32, and bounded calls retain their qualified routes.
- Add an affine split-scan composite for long H4/H8/H16 unbounded prefill. The main, affine-map, device-scan, and correction stages are generated, SHA-verified, and selected additively; ordinary direct, checkpoint, bounded, and row-strided-beta routes remain unchanged.
- Use one-warp split
