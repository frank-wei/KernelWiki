## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, the kernels in this PR **outperform** the relevant **SOTA baselines at the time of merge** on the **vast majority of performance benchmark shapes**, matching them otherwise.

Closes #5795 (first round: the host-only steps; the kernel-level steps are listed below as follow-ups).

## What changed

Host-only consolidation of the generated Sage block-sparse attention family. No kernel body changes: every compiled kernel on
`sm_100a`, `sm_103a` and `sm_120a` is SASS-identical to the base tree (see evidence).

- **One launcher for the 25 SM120 specializations.** `sm_120a/cake_sage_block_sparse_attention_binding.cu` is compiled into
  every SM120 module with the kernel symbol passed as `-DCAKE_SAGE_SM120_KERNEL=...` by the JIT loader. The 25 identical
  538-line bindings are removed.
- **SM100 and SM103 share one source pair per stage** (`sm_100a/`): `quantize_v` was byte-identical; the shared-memory
  address conversion in `quantize_qk_vamax` and `attention`, and the sm_100a-only FMA-polynomial `exp2` on a quarter of the
  softmax exponentials, sit under `#if __CUDA_ARCH__ == 1000`. `sm_103a/` is removed. JIT spec names are unchanged for both
  architect
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
