## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`cake_quant_lora_gemm` fuses a quantized GEMM (FP8 E4M3 or NVFP4) with a runtime BF16 low-rank LoRA term and BF16 output. The family ships 11 program variants: 6 FP8 and 5 NVFP4. This PR updates the two SM90 FP8 variants, `fp8_sm90` (form `acc2`) and `fp8_sm90_hint` (form `acc2_hint`), with a faster mainloop schedule. The peer CTA of the 2-CTA cluster now issues its own A box and LoRA D slab, while the W tile and its LoRA B slab stay multicast. One elected lane per consumer warpgroup releases a ring stage. Ring slot and parity come from running counters. Output is bit-identical. The baseline is the previous Cake version of the same two programs, already shipped in FlashInfer. Against it, the new programs take 0.975 to 0.985 of the previous kernel time (table below).

NVIDIA H100 80GB HBM3 (SXM, 700 W), sm_90a, CUDA 13.0. CUPTI kernel time, cold L2, medians of paired rounds. Back to back: GPU throttled at about 1330 MHz. Cooled: pause between rounds. Range in parentheses is over all runs of the shape.
