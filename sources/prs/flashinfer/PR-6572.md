## Summary

Fixes #6570

`flashinfer.experimental.cake_moe_grouped_gemm.grouped_gemm_wgrad` sometimes returned wrong 128 x 128 blocks of `dW[e]` for a group with fewer than 64 rows. Each wrong block equals the group's own rows plus the rows that follow it, which belong to the next group.

This PR regenerates the four weight-gradient main kernels. Each CTA now owns a ring of nine tensor-map pairs. At a group switch the loader edits a pair that no in-flight TMA load can still read. The fix adds no wait. `grouped_gemm_fwd` and `grouped_gemm_dgrad` are not changed.

On the shipped kernels, the #6570 reproduction (256 groups x 32 rows, N=1024, K=6144) gives 35/300 wrong calls with bf16 output and 300/300 with fp32 output. With this PR, both give 0/300 on GB300 and on B200. In a paired A/B, every shape measured on GB300 and B200 stays above the 0.97 bar; the lowest old/new median is 0.9768.

## Cause

The loader (one elected lane per CTA) kept two TMA tensor maps per CTA: A for the rows of `G` and B for the rows of `X`. At every group switch it rewrote their row extent in place (`tensormap.replace.tile.global_dim`, then `fence.proxy.tensormap::generic.release` / `.acquire`).
