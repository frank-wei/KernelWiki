## 📌 Description

Part of #4254. Implements the overlap requested in #6304 (assumptions stated below where the issue thread has not confirmed a choice).

Adds `flashinfer.comm.QkvNormRopeExchangeOverlap`: one prepared session that fuses the attention front end of a
sequence-parallel (Ulysses) layer — the BF16 QKV projection, per-head RMSNorm of Q and K, interleaved RoPE from a
per-token cos/sin table, and the head exchange — into a single call per layer. The Cake backend stores every
destination's head band straight from the GEMM epilogue into that rank's symmetric receive slot over NVLink and
publishes per-chunk readiness words, so the exchange overlaps the projection instead of following it; a one-CTA wait
kernel on a consumer stream acquires the words and the caller's stream joins it.

* Architectures: SM90 (WGMMA producer), SM100 and SM103 (tcgen05 producer; 2-CTA pair tile for `block_n = 256` and
  rows of at least 4096 tokens). Single-node NVLink groups of 2, 4 or 8 ranks, `head_dim = 128`, BF16 operands,
  fp32 cos/sin table.
* Layouts: `"bshd"` returns `(q, k, v)` views of `[B, S_global, H_local, D]`; `"fused"` returns one
  `[B, S_global, H_local, 3 * D]` payload. Outputs
