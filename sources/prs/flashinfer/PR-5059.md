## 📌 Description

Support contiguous non-absorbed MLA prefill with separate Q/K=192 and V/O=128 in `prims_ts`, targeting BF16 and FP8. Reuse paired 128-row kernels with 128+64 QK staging, independent V/output sizing, and improved TMEM/softmax overlap. Serialize correction for single-accumulator schedules to prevent a PV overwrite race exposed by faster softmax.
