### Summary
As reported in #3484, the FA2 `BatchPrefill` kernels (paged + ragged) are  slower with an FP8 KV cache than with BF16. In the FP8 K/V case, the cache was being dequantized to BF16 in registers on every tile and then fed to the same BF16 MMA. That's pure ALU/shuffle overhead with no matmul speedup.

This PR removes the per-fragment shuffles by **dequantizing each FP8 K/V tile once into a BF16 shared-memory staging buffer** (a shuffle-free, vectorized pass), then reading it with the standard 16-bit `ldmatrix` path. The tensor cores stay fed, and most of the FP8↔BF16 gap closes.

Also includes small changes that fixes a bug where to microbenchmark fails due to small workspace allocation, and increased coverage in unit testing.

### Approach

- After the FP8 K/V tile lands in smem, a vectorized pass converts it to BF16 in a single time-shared staging buffer (K is repacked+consumed by `compute_qk` before V is repacked+consumed by `compute_sfm_v`, so they share one buffer).
