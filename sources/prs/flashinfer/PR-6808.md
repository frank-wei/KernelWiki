The Cake backend of FlashInfer's Mamba2 SSD combined chunk-scan prefill, `SSDCombined(backend="cake")`, ships three state data types (bf16, f16, f32) x two sequence layouts (batched, varlen) x these program forms: exact-scan (fused one-launch, preprocess+main two-launch, metadata+main two-launch, in-kernel-table one-launch, tail-wave pair, plus the no-ring exact-scan forms a route can select), look-back chunk-parallel and three-phase chunk-parallel. The family ships 93 generated device sources across `sm_100a`, `sm_103a` and `sm_107a`.
