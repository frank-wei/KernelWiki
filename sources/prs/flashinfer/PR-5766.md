## Summary

Regenerates the fused routing-gate family of the experimental DeepGEMM-family package (#5523) - `flashinfer.experimental.deepgemm_mega_gate`, public API `flashinfer.mega_gate.prepare_mega_gate` - for **SM100a (B200, 148 SMs) and SM103a (GB300, 152 SMs)**. One scheduling-only change, bitwise identical to native DeepGEMM `39d8c4ca` on every shape (expert indices and normalized weights): on the M = 1 cluster split-K reduction route, the four gate warps of every expert-group leader score, bias and rank their 32 experts right after the split-order reduction and publish per-warp top-k records (ranking, unbiased score, expert id) instead of the 128 raw scores; the final ranker merges the 3 x 4 x 6 records under the same total order (ranking descending, expert id ascending) and reads the winner's unbiased FP32 score from its record.
