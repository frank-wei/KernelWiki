## Summary

Adds `flashinfer.cake_rmsnorm_train`: a generated BF16 RMSNorm **training** operator for SM100a (B200), SM103a (B300 / GB300) and SM107a (R200) with

* forward `y = bf16(x * rstd * w)` plus the FP32 reciprocal RMS `rstd` needed by the backward (optionally fused with a residual add: `h_new = bf16(x + residual)` is normalized and returned),
* backward `dx` (BF16) and `dw` (FP32) in **one launch**, with a **bitwise deterministic** `dw` whose reduction order is a pure function of `(T, device)`,
* a `torch.autograd.Function` (`CakeRMSNormFunction`) and a module-free helper (`cake_rmsnorm`) that save only `(x, w, rstd)`.

`flashinfer.norm.rmsnorm` is an inference kernel (no `rstd`, no backward); training frameworks fall back to `torch.compile` / eager autograd / Liger. This PR is the training counterpart requested in #5679.
