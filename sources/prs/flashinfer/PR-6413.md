## Summary

Re-export of the `deepgemm_dense_mqa` family (dense 32-head FP8 / FP4 and 64-head FP8 MQA logits, paged FP8 MQA logits) from the producer tip of the third convergence round against the stock DeepGEMM kernels (`fp8_mqa_logits` / `fp8_paged_mqa_logits`, sgl-deep-gemm 0.2.0), rebuilt on `main` after the v9 single-launch routes landed there. Catalog schema `dense_mqa.v9` -> `dense_mqa.v10` (every v9 field name kept; two fields are reshaped: `policy.kv_alignment` is keyed by precision then head count (v9: head count only) and each route's `kv_alignment` becomes a per-architecture map `{arch: divisor}` (v9: one integer divisor per route); new per-route `kv_min`, the published single-launch precedence, the per-device `fused_qle2048_max_kv` bound, three static-window forms).

1. **32-head FP8 dense logits, 1 < Q <= 128 -- fused single-launch route `fp8:qle128:short`.** One kernel computes the per-token metadata and the logits (the metadata producer + logits pair disappears).
