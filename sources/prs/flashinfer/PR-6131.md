## Summary

`csrc/gdn/cake` gains the Qwen3.5 serving rows the shipped layout lacked:

* **BF16-state T=1 decode for every Qwen3.5 per-rank geometry at any batch** (35B-A3B TP1 H16/HV32, TP2 H8/HV16, TP4 H4/HV8; 397B-A17B TP2 H8/HV32, TP4 H4/HV16, TP8 H2/HV8).
