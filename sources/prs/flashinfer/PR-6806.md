## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Cake's `selective_state_update` backend ships thirteen programs: BF16 and FP32 state, single-token, MTP and checkpoint-cache forms, and headdim 128 and 64. This PR changes seven of them: the five BF16 single-token programs, the headdim-64 paired-row program and the horizontal MTP program. In each, every consumer warp now synchronizes before its elected lane releases a shared-memory state stage, and latency is unchanged against the previous Cake version.

Latency in µs, back to back / cooled (mean of the per-allocation medians over 8 input allocations, two alternating runs per side), on NVIDIA GB300 (sm_103a) with CUDA 13.3:

| program | batch | heads | groups | head dim | state dim | tokens | before | after | speedup |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BF16 single-token, direct | 32 | 16 | 16 | 128 | 128 | 1 | 10.68 / 10.69 | 10.70 / 10.69 | 0.999x / 0.999x |
| BF16 single-token, ratio 8 | 64 | 64 | 8 | 128 | 128 | 1 | 50.74 / 50.71 | 50.71 / 50.68 | 1.001x / 1.001x |
| BF16 single-token, ratio 8 saturated | 128 | 64 | 8 | 128 | 128 | 1 | 86.53 / 86.61 | 86.54 / 86.54 | 1.000x / 1.001x |
| BF16 single-token, ratio 16 | 32 | 128 | 8 | 128 | 128 | 1 | 50.70 / 50.71 | 50.67
