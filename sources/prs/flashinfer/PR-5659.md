## Summary

The native KDA prefill facade (`recurrent_kda(backend="cake")` on a BF16 state pool, `csrc/kda/flashkda_generated_*`) ships the fused-M128 and persistent-M128 BF16 bodies from before the block-inverse fix that the prepared BF16 export already carries. In those bodies the 8-row block inverse is composed from the packed 4-row inverse fragment **before** the last 4-row product was written back to it. The error enters at token 8 of every chunk and grows with `beta_i * beta_j * <k_i, k_j>`, so it only shows up when a head combines beta close to 1, a decay rate `exp(A_log)` close to 1 and keys that share a direction inside a chunk. Isotropic random inputs never reach that regime, which is why the existing tests passed; a real Kimi K3 layer-44 prefill (SGLang #34299) read rel L2 63 on the output and 49 on the final state, with non-finite values.
