## Summary

`flashinfer.experimental.minimax_h3_varlen_attention` (Cake backend, SM100a/SM103a BF16 packed-varlen attention), round 13 on top of #6328. The host planner now selects one of three bitwise-identical programs per plan:

- **Dynamic whole-unit ticket scheduling** (`DYN_SCHED` + ticket prefetch) for plans with >= 2 waves, >= 8192 stage-weighted KV blocks on every cluster and either a non-makespan-optimal LPT order or >= 64 units per cluster (7 contract rows per architecture): the persistent 2-CTA grid draws its next unit from a per-cluster atomic ticket; the ticket of unit k+1 is drawn at the head of unit k and consumed at its tail.
- **Publish-tail cut** (`SREAD_UNIFIED`) for the remaining SM100a plans: stage 0 waits the other stage's score-read barrier before every P store, removing a uniform compare + branch from the path between the last exp2 and the P stores. SM103a static plans keep the round-12 program.
