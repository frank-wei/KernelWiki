## Summary

Round 2 of the Cake Mamba2 SSD combined prefill (`SSDCombined(backend="cake")`), regenerated from Cake `7f1aeddb006` (main `ebb5a2f917a` + the round) through the export front door `tools/export_cake_mamba_ssd_combined.py` (no hand edits of generated files). Round 1 is #6051 (FlashInfer main `2a57c19bace5`).

- **Calls shorter than one chunk.** The runner zero-pads `x`/`B`/`C`/`dt`/`z` and the output staging to one 128-row chunk when the total token count is < 128 (batched and varlen), so the generated host's fixed 128-row TMA token box always covers a full chunk; `final_states` and the visible `out` rows are unchanged. New rows: varlen (1,), (8,), (127,), (64, 60); batched 2×50, 1×1; a long→short NaN-injection sequence in one process.
- **`seq_idx` guard and empty sequences.** The preprocess takes a runner-owned `preprocess_status` word: out-of-range or non-monotonic packed-sequence ids are clamped and flagged instead of writing out of bounds; ids without tokens get an empty chunk range and pass their initial state through to `final_states`. No host-side device sync on the launch path; `seq_idx_status()` reads the flag back (tests / debugging only).
