## What this PR does

Makes the five Cake-generated backends dispatch and pass on Rubin R200 (CC 10.7, 212 SMs), fixes what the Rubin bring-up found, and gives the BF16-state T=1 GDN decode route its own sm_107a band table with the records it routes to. The four non-concat backends (DSv3 routing, BGMV, GDN decode/prefill, packed KDA) make no sm_100a/sm_103a kernel-source change — their generated CUDA and manifests for those architectures are byte-identical (cubin-identity gate on a GDN-decode representative on B200 and GB300 at every round, plus source-diff scope). The concat-MLA-K backend is re-exported here (new content-addressed body + v5 manifest); its output stays bitwise-identical to the previous body.
