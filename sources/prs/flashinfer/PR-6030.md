## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

Follow-up to #6003 for two items recorded on #5825 (part of #5768): the softplus threshold raised in review (thread on `cake_selective_state_update_mtp_short` / `dynamic_checkpoint0`) and the AOT registration of the family.

## What changed

- **Thresholded softplus in the regenerated `mtp_short` and `dynamic` programs.** Both computed `log1pf(expf(dt))` for every `dt` when softplus was requested, while the FlashInfer reference (`thresholded_softplus`) and the other Cake programs apply the identity above 20. Above 20 the identity is exact in FP32; without the guard `expf` overflows past ~88 and the state and output became inf/NaN. The two programs are regenerated with the guard (`if (dt_value <= 20.0f)`); the other three regenerated programs and the five kept BF16 STP programs are unchanged in behaviour (the regenerated sources carry new content digests because the generator revision moved; see Validation).
