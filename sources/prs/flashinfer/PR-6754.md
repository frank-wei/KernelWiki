## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Cake's gated delta rule (GDN) family has prefill variants and decode variants. Prefill variants cover five recurrent-state dtypes: fp32, bf16, fp16, fp8 e4m3 and fp8 e5m2. The fp16 and fp8 states exist only in the DV-split initial-state form. Prefill also covers two activation dtypes (bf16, fp16) and the plain, initial-state, final-state, DV-split, single-chunk and gate-stream forms. Decode variants cover two state dtypes (fp32, bf16) with bf16 activations, two layouts (pretranspose, nontranspose), and T=1 serving or multi-token (MTP, T ≥ 2) verify / update / checkpoint forms.

This PR adds 129 new sm_107a records for Rubin:
- 107 prefill records across 17 kernel templates. They include all five state dtypes, both activation dtypes, and the plain, initial-state, DV-split, single-chunk and gate-stream forms.
- 22 decode records across 8 templates. They cover fp32 T=1 in both layouts, fp32 MTP at T=2 and T=4 for batch ≠ 4, and bf16-state MTP.

It also adds the Cake sm_107a packed KDA T=1 kernels with their JIT binding. These come in tile-128 and tile-64 register-pipeline forms and use bf16 activations and bf16 state.

Speedups, given as seed 17 / seed 29:
- **GDN decode:** geomean 1.
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
