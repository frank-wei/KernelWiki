## What changes

`flashinfer/experimental/cake_dsa_train` is regenerated from its Cake producer on top of current `main`, i.e. after
#5737 (SM107 program, strided / packed GLM-5.2 trainer layouts, packed fp32 dKV accumulation) and #5865 (single-pass key
ranges for packed multi-segment rows, fused varlen index glue) merged: every feature of those two PRs is kept, emitted
through the lean delivery. The package shrinks from 37 generated files / 50,957 lines plus a 2,552-line
backend to 12 generated files / 14,814 lines plus a 2,073-line backend
(`git diff --shortstat` against the base: 46 files, +3,189 / -40,647, with or without rename detection).

- **One source per stage.** The `csrc/cake_dsa_h64_train/sm_100a`, `sm_103a` and `sm_107a` trees are replaced by six
  programs (`fwd`, `bwd_delta`, `bwd_main`, `bwd_compact`, `bwd_main_pass`, `bwd_cast`), each one kernel file plus one
  binding file, compiled for SM100, SM103 and SM107 from the same text. The three stages whose shared-memory base idiom differed by
  architecture
