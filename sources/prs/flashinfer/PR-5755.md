## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
> Developed by the **CAKE team**, the kernels in this PR **outperform** the relevant **SOTA baselines at the time of merge** on the **vast majority of performance benchmark shapes**, matching them otherwise.

Adds a generated program family to the Cake Kimi-K3 NVFP4 SiTU experts (`cutlass_fused_moe(..., activation_type=ActivationType.Situ, tp_size=8, backend="cake")`) for the 16384-token shape on SM100 (B200) and SM103 (B300), shipped for both architectures:

- **Scale-factor writer (`kimi_k3_nvfp4_situ_sfb_shuffle_n128`).** A per-tile kernel (grid `(max_tiles, 1, 1)`, 128 threads) that writes, for every (N-tile, K-step), the 4 KB shared-memory image of the routed B scale factors that the FC1 mainloop consumes, into a new workspace field `sfb_shuffled` (`max_tiles * K_tiles * 4096` bytes, appended last to the 16384-token layout only).
- **Pre-shuffled-copy FC1 (`kimi_k3_nvfp4_situ_fc1_n128_l3sf16_c7`).** The tile-N128 FC1 program loads that image with one 4 KB TMA copy per K-step on the A/scale-factor producer warp (transaction barrier, same pattern as the A scale factors) instead of gathering the scale factors with per-thread asynchronous copies and shuffling them in place between
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
