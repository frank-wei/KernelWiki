## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
The `cake_gdn` package in `csrc/gdn/cake` ships 226 kernel records: 83 decode and 143 prefill. The prefill records cover 22 schedules: full-DV and DV-split chunked prefill, with and without an initial state, fp32 / bf16 / fp8 recurrent state, bf16 or fp16 activations, and gate-stream and single-chunk forms. This PR re-exports the prefill bodies. 84 of the 143 prefill records move to 14 new bodies (16 programs: 7 for sm_100a, 9 for sm_103a). The other 59 prefill records and all 83 decode records keep their shipped bodies byte for byte. Four multi-chunk DV-split programs now fold the per-site value-split warp guard into the prologue's warp-uniform register, and every re-rendered program now has the tcgen05 thread-sync fences that the generator emits around its barriers. Outputs are bit-identical to the previous bodies on every measured shape.

The baseline is the previous Cake version of these kernels: the `cake_gdn` prefill bodies on FlashInfer main at 5b0f0ca9. Speedup is previous time / this PR's time, so values above 1 mean this PR is faster.
