## 📌 Description

### Motivation

`chunk_gated_delta_rule(..., backend="cake_gdn")` without CP resolved its per-sequence lengths on the
host: `_run_cake_gdn_prefill -> _cake_gdn_prefill_seq_lens -> _cake_gdn_host_ints` ran
`cu_seqlens.detach().cpu().tolist()` (one full device-to-host synchronization) and cached the result
under an identity key `(device, data_ptr, version or None, numel)` that also required the weak-referenced
tensor to be the *same object*. A second identity-keyed cache (`_cake_gdn_i32`) held int32 copies of
int64 metadata. Consequences:

- vLLM pays one synchronization per prefill step (the engine passes a fresh int32
  `prefill_query_start_loc` every step; 36 layers share one resolution), and CUDA Graph capture needed one
  eager pre-resolution or raised `CakeGDNUnsupportedError("... requires one eager cu_seqlens resolution
  before CUDA Graph capture")`.
- The default SM100 GDN prefill (`backend="auto"`, CuTe-DSL `chunk_gated_delta_rule_sm100`) has no host
  synchronization, so under concurrency the Cake route lost step overlap. In an internal vLLM measurement
