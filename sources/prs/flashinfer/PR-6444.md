## Summary

`concat_mla_kv_quant_fp8`, `backend="cake"`: the four generated Cake programs are re-exported with the BF16 -> f32 widening done by bit placement (`and 0xFFFF0000` / `shl 16`, the same form the hand-written specialized kernel uses) instead of `cvt.f32.bf16` (Cake CAKE-1089 round 12). Kernel files change (new content ids under `csrc/cake_concat_mla_kv_quant_fp8/`, the previous eight removed) and the loader's rendered `MODULES` / `ROUTES` table follows; the public API, the head-group plan, the dispatch surface, `backend="auto"` and the tests are unchanged. Numerics are unchanged: the saturating e4m3 cast is byte-exact with the torch reference on every contract row (68/68 per architecture, B200 and GB300).

## Why

Round 11's ladders left one lever with a consistent positive sign: `bits` at +0.4-0.5 % on the 12-head routes on B200 and GB300, neutral elsewhere. Round 12 confirms it on the delivered FlashInfer path (CUPTI cold-L2 medians, 15 interleaved rounds, shared output buffers, all 75 contract rows; B200 `nsc-svg-slurm-1-gpu-12`, GB300 `nvl72d334-T12`):

| ratio (geomean, min-max, rows > 1) | B200 (sm_100a) | GB300 (sm_103a) |
|---|---|---|
