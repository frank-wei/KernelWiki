## Summary

`backend="cake"` DSv4 sparse-MLA (SM100 / SM103), performance-only follow-up of the round-5 release, two changes: the
two FP8 H128 persistent programs (`fp8_h128_prefill_source_persistent`, `fp8_h128_prefill_source_persistent_uniform`,
both architectures) trim their kernel prologue to a single cluster rendezvous, and the BF16 SWA128 single-CTA program
(`bf16_swa128_single_cta`, Cake source `flashinfer_blackwell_trtllm_sparse_mla_dsv4_seed_bf16_swa128_single_cta`, both
architectures) runs a two-chunk online softmax over its two key halves. Sparse-validity semantics, the host contract
(`flashinfer/mla/cake_dsv4.py`, `flashinfer/mla/_core.py`) and every other program are unchanged. The prologue change
keeps the FP8 output bits; the SWA128 change keeps the precision class (f32 accumulation, exact exp2/rcp paths, masks)
and moves output bits within the BF16 tolerance.
