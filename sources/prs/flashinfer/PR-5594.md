regenerated 10.0 / 10.3 programs, under one three-architecture export lock (16 shapes x 3 architectures, 48 sealed receipts).

  capacity (212 SMs), and the short-item cluster program; registry records carry `route`, `splits`, `tail`, `cluster_capacity`.
