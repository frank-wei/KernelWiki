## Purpose

Fix repeated B200 main failures in `(B200) MoE Refactor Integration TEMPORARY Shard 3` for the two Qwen3-30B NVFP4 CUTLASS configs.

MRV2 marks padded MoE rows with `topk_ids == -1`. The CUTLASS permutation builder counted those rows out of the expert work but left their `input_permutation` slots uninitialized. `shuffle_rows` then dereferenced the garbage source indices and both TP ranks crashed with `CUDBG_EXCEPTION_WARP_ILLEGAL_ADDRESS` in `shuffleInputRowsKernel<__nv_bfloat16>` during the first profile forward.

This PR:
- writes explicit invalid sentinels for padded/non-local routes in both permutation maps;
- bounds-checks `shuffle_rows` source indices and produces zero rows for sentinels;
- limits NVFP4 and MXFP4 expert activation quantization to rows below `expert_offsets[num_experts]`, preventing padded rows from racing valid block-scale writes or reading unwritten intermediates;
- adds focused permutation/shuffle coverage and an end-to-end NVFP4/MXFP4 regression.
