## Overview

Tune CUDA FP16/BF16 `moe_fused_mul_sum` launches: cap `BLOCK_K` at 4096 and use 8 warps on SM75, 16 on newer GPUs. FP32, small hidden sizes, and non-CUDA platforms keep their existing settings.

## Claims

- Reduce serial hidden-tile iterations with a small launch-only change; retain the kernel and one-CTA-per-token grid.
- Across the 30 shapes below, geometric-mean speedups over the previous kernel configuration are:

| GPU | Dtype | Cold | Hot / no-flush |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| RTX 2080 Ti (SM75) | FP16 | 1.18x | 1.27x |
| RTX 3090 (SM86) | BF16 | 1.17x | 1.28x |
| RTX 4090 (SM89) | BF16 | 1.27x | 1.37x |
| B300 (SM103) | BF16 | 1.51x | 1.63x |
| RTX PRO 6000 (SM120) | BF16 | 1.69x | 2.37x |
| GB10 (SM121) | BF16 | 1.32x | 1.85x |

## Validation

- `.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/kernels/moe/test_moe_fused_mul_sum.py -q`: **114 passed** on SM121, including FP16/BF16/FP32, partial tiles, invalid experts and padding.
- All pre-commit hooks on the changed files and manual `mypy-3.12` passed.
- Five randomized rounds; median latency per round, then median across rounds. Cold: 15 samples/round, 512 MiB flush before each timed kernel (32 MiB on SM75). Hot: repeated CUDA
