## Purpose

Fuse Qwen4Exp's BF16 HC down projection and SiLU for NVIDIA decode batches up to 48 tokens, with the existing unfused path for larger batches. This is separate from #58706 (ROCm) and #53909 (other HC operations).

The CuTe DSL kernel adapts vLLM's `ll_bf16` dot-product and split-K GEMM paths. It applies SiLU in the output epilogue, preserves the `ll_bf16` BF16 rounding boundary, and skips the 12 unused padding columns.

## Microbenchmarks

GB300, FlashInfer CUPTI with CUDA graph and cold L2; median latency in µs. Unfused is the Qwen4Exp serving GEMM (`shape_dynamic_skinny_gemm` at M=1/2/4/8, otherwise `F.linear`) + `hc_silu`.

| 4 | 6.75 | 6.02 | 1.12× |
| 8 | 8.29 | 5.54 | 1.50× |
