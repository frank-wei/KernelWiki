## Purpose
his PR continues the libtorch stable ABI migration (see #26946) for vLLM Marlin CUDA kernels by moving Marlin host ops (marlin_gemm, gptq_marlin_repack, awq_marlin_repack, and marlin_int4_fp8_preprocess), generated template kernels (sm75/sm80/sm89), and shared device headers into csrc/libtorch_stable/quantization/marlin/, registering them on _C_stable_libtorch and removing them from legacy _C.
