## Purpose
When serving with expert-parallelism enabled, the MoE inputs and intermediate states are padded along the token dimension to handle the worst case (i.e. all tokens routed to a single rank; see https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/vllm/model_executor/layers/fused_moe/prepare_finalize/deepep_v2.py#L32-L33).

Most MoE kernels have internal logic to ignore such padding tokens during GEMM execution; however, the MoE activation kernels do not currently ignore padding tokens. This PR introduces a padding-aware variant of the swiglu limit activation.
