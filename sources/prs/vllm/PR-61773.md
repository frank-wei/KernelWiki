## Overview

Skip the per-layer `a1q_scale.t().contiguous()` copy between per-token-group FP8 quantization and the FlashInfer TRTLLM fp8-block MoE experts by emitting the scales in the layout the experts consume. This is pure plumbing on top of the quant kernel's existing `column_major_scales` mode — no kernel changes. ~2% TPOT improvement at concurrency 4 on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8 (TP4+EP).

## Claims

- Removes one `direct_copy_kernel` per MoE layer per decode step (24/step -> 0 on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next) from the critical path.
- ~2% TPOT p50 improvement at concurrency 4 on Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8, TP4+EP on 4xGB300; parity at concurrency 1 (details below).
- Bitwise-identical expert inputs: the scale bytes are the same values, just allocated in the final physical layout, so there is no accuracy impact.
- Python-only change (+77/-14 across 5 files, mostly the test).
