## Summary

In the ROCm sparse-MLA backend, `forward_mqa` builds `q = torch.cat([ql_nope, q_pe], dim=-1)` once per layer per forward pass. On DeepSeek-V3.2 (61 sparse-MLA layers on the decode path) this adds up to **61 fresh tensor allocations and 61 `aten::CatArrayBatchedCopy` kernel launches per decode step**, ≈ 360 µs at MC=4 (≈ 4% of a decode step measured at TP=4, ISL=1000, OSL=100 on MI355X).

The fused `ops.concat_mla_q(ql_nope, q_pe, q_out)` already exists in `vllm/_custom_ops.py` and is already used by `FlashMLASparseImpl.forward_mqa` at `vllm/v1/attention/backends/mla/flashmla_sparse.py:1028`. This PR plugs it into `ROCMAiterMLASparseImpl` against a preallocated workspace buffer sized `(max_num_batched_tokens, num_heads, head_size)`, so:

- No per-layer allocation on the decode hot path.
- Output address is stable across CUDA graph capture/replay (same pattern `FlashMLASparseImpl` uses today).
- One fused kernel instead of one ATen cat.

The patch is numerically identical to the `torch.cat` it replaces (no change in GSM8K, see below).
