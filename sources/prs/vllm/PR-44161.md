## Summary
- optimize the DeepSeek V4 CuteDSL C4A FlashMLA sparse FP8-with-scale packed KV compressor layout
- optimize the C128A split sparse compressor by matching the 128-row tile with fewer warps and shorter final reductions
- keep the change scoped to the legacy FlashMLA packed FP8 + UE8M0 scale + BF16 RoPE layout; no FP8/BF16 full-cache kernels are added

## Why
Mixed decode/prefill batches with tokens from different sequences regressed versus Triton because the C4A CuteDSL packed-layout kernel used too many lanes for too little per-lane work, and C128A used more warps than needed for the 128-row compression window. The new mapping increases per-lane vector work for C4A and reduces C128A reduction overhead.
