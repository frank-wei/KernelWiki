## Improve the perf of triton compressor kernel

This PR adds two kinds of cutedsl compressor kernels. One is fused kernel that fuses compression and norm+rope+quant. Another is the split kernel where the compression and norm+rope+quant are in seperate kernels.
Based on the performance, the compressor will choose the fused kernel for C4 and the split kernel for C128.
