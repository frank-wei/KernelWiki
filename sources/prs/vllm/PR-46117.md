## Summary

Numerically-equivalent Triton-kernel optimizations for the native MXFP8 path of MiniMax-M3 on
AMD MI355X (CDNA4 / gfx950). Math is unchanged (`tl.dot_scaled`, fp32 accumulate; outputs
numerically identical to the current kernels, tol 6e-2). **AMD/ROCm-only**: both kernels are
gated on `current_platform.is_rocm() and supports_mx()` (gfx95x); on other platforms the oracle
selects the existing paths, so nothing here affects non-ROCm. Validated at **TP=4 and TP=8**.

Two changes, both pure GEMM-launcher improvements (no new env flags, no new quant kernel):

    **Decode (small M)**: narrow BLOCK_N and larger BLOCK_K for better occupancy and weight-bandwidth efficiency.
