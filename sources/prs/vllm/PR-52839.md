## Purpose

Cosmetic refactor of cp related attention ops.

* consolidates dcp attn ops across `ops/common.py`, `ops/dcp_utils.py` and `ops/dcp_alltoall.py` into `ops/dcp.py`
* moves pcp attn ops from `model_executor/layers/attention/pcp.py` to `vllm/v1/attention/ops/pcp.py`
* extracts symmem related capability into `ops/cp_common.py`, will be used by prefill pcp fused ops in later diffs

End state in attention/ops/
- `cp_common.py`: symmetric-memory probing, capability gates, and the direct CP workspace base.
- `dcp.py`: dcp lse combine, q gather, kv gather, and manager
- `pcp.py`: pcp kv gather, pcp_dcp_combine
