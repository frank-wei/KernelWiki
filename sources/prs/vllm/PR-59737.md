## Overview

The FP32 router GEMM supports programmatic dependent launch (PDL), but fused MoE `topkGating` uses an ordinary launch. Enable PDL for the consumer on SM90+ with CUDA 12+, allowing it to start early and wait before reading producer-dependent data.
<img width="842" height="150" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/05cdfe4d-2bdc-40d5-9559-a74492884ce3" />


## Claims

- On GB200, the M=4 MiniMax-M3 router-to-gating CUDA Graph chain saves **0.486 us (-9.12%)** with hot weights and **0.466 us (-7.77%)** with rotating weights.
- In ordinary MiniMax-M3 aggregated serving, mean TPOT improves **0.61–1.16%** at request concurrency 1/16/32. All three paired runs improve for both tested input lengths at these concurrencies. C4 is effectively unchanged.
- Pure-prefill C16/C32 mean TTFT regresses **0.54%/0.49%**. The results support a modest decode benefit on this setup; broader workload and platform coverage remains open.
