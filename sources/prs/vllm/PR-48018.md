## Background

This is the first sub-PR split out from the large draft PR [#47576](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/47576), which presents the full ReplaySSM design across Mamba2 and Gated DeltaNet for both standard and speculative decode. To keep review tractable, this PR lands only the first stage, Mamba2 standard decode. ReplaySSM caches recent SSM inputs instead of writing the recurrent state back to HBM on every step. It is a collaboration between [Dao AI Lab](https://dao-lab.ai) and NVIDIA, and the full derivation and figures are in the [blog post](https://dao-lab.ai/blog/2026/replayssm/). AI assistance was used for parts of this work, and the authors have reviewed every changed line.

## Core concept

SSM decode is memory-bound. Each step loads and stores a `(nheads, d, n)` recurrent state, and that per-step state store dominates HBM traffic. ReplaySSM keeps a small ring buffer of recent inputs together with a periodic checkpoint state.
