## Purpose

The MoE gate of MiniMax-M2 needs to perform GEMM in FP32 and requires converting the input to FP32, see [router_logits](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/v0.18.1rc0/vllm/model_executor/models/minimax_m2.py#L132). Therefore, in low-concurrency scenarios, up to 3 kernels may be launched in the worst case:
-  the first one is BF16-to-FP32 conversion
- and the second and third are the two kernels of `split-K` GEMM.


![img_v3_02108_87c02d83-ccac-4465-8f0e-4b28a96fc7eg](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c7546a7-7644-4181-97d2-c2ffc5cc44f0)


This PR implements a fused kernel referencing the `dsv3_router_gemm` implementation, which yields a significant performance improvement under low-concurrency conditions.

![img_v3_02108_ad60c4ed-dc70-4432-9b7b-9730440ced6g](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9f374096-8ca2-4e36-bd59-8c1835832b64)
