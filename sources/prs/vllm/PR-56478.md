## Purpose

Avoid the odd-row performance cliff in per-token-group 8-bit quantization while preserving the existing launch policy for even row counts.

We identified this while investigating unexpectedly poor TPOT (time per output token) with Gemma 4 31B FP8 at TP8 on H100. The reproduction traced about 35 ms of extra time in a 13,359-token prefill forward pass to activation quantization, enough to erase the roughly 30 ms saved by FP8 GEMMs. At TP8, the down-projection input has width 2688: odd row counts launch 16-thread blocks and make quantization roughly 4× slower than the neighboring even case. The width-5376 inputs also take a roughly 2× slower path.

The current heuristic selects one group per block when the total group count is odd, launching only 16 threads. This change selects block geometry using the next even row count, then uses ceil-div and bounds checks for the actual groups. No tensors are padded or copied. All threads still participate in block and PDL synchronization, and the bounds check is compiled out for full blocks.
