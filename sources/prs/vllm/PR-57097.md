## Purpose

Each QSA layer of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prepares its inputs with three launches before the sparse attention kernel:

1. `fused_qk_rmsnorm_rope_gate`: main-attention Q/K RMSNorm, RoPE and the gate split.
2. `qsa_pre_indexer`: indexer Q/K norm and RoPE, compressed-K cache and raw-key ring.
3. `reshape_and_cache_flash`: main K/V cache write.

This PR appends the main-attention work to the `qsa_pre_indexer` launch and renames it to `qsa_prepare`. After the indexer programs, one program per (token, Q or KV head) normalizes and rotates Q/K, copies the gate and writes K/V straight into the paged cache: BF16, or FP8 scaled by the layer's `k_scale`/`v_scale` the way `reshape_and_cache_flash` does. Two launches and the round trip of K through an intermediate tensor go away. The fused path runs for every batch (decode, prefill, mixed) whenever both fused kernels are available; otherwise the existing path runs.

`vllm/model_executor/layers/fused_qk_norm_rope.py` is unchanged; the logic lives in `vllm/models/qwen4_exp/nvidia/ops/qsa_prepare.py` (renamed from `qsa_pre_indexer.py`).
