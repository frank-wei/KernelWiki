## Motivation

GLM-Image's transformer block (hidden 4096, 30 blocks, 32 heads x head_dim
128, 30 steps, true CFG = 2 forwards/step) still runs its adaLN chain in
eager pieces — 242 bare aten `layer_norm` launches per step plus a
hand-written modulate/gate chain around them:

- `GlmImageAdaLayerNormZero` runs bare `nn.LayerNorm` (no affine) plus a
  hand-written `norm * (1 + scale) + shift` for both the image and text
  streams — 4 aten kernels per stream per block;
- the qk-norm is `nn.LayerNorm(128)` per head on `(1, S, 32, 128)`: aten's
  `vectorized_layer_norm_kernel` spends a full 128-thread block per
  128-element row (warp 0 does 4 elements per lane, warps 1-3 idle), and
  the two launches cost 301 us per block at the real shape — **10.1% of
  denoise GPU time** in the profiler trace;
- the final `GlmImageAdaLayerNormContinuous` repeats the LN+modulate
  pattern once per forward;
- the ff gate `hidden + ff_out * gate` is another eager pair per stream.

This PR fuses each site into one Triton kernel that is **numerically
re-engineered to be rounding-faithful to the eager chain, step for step**,
so it is bit-exact (`torch.equal`, full-image md5-identical) and wired on
