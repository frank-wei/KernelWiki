## Motivation

LingBot World V2 causal blocks materialize FP32 LayerNorm intermediates and launch separate modulation operations on BF16 activations. The self-attention residual also computes a normalized output that is discarded before camera conditioning and a second normalization.

Fusing these exact arithmetic chains reduces saved-client latency by **at least 2.98% in both fixed native thirty-chunk WebSocket ABBA groups** on one H200. The workload generates **357 frames at 832×480**, with unchanged raw RGB frames and byte-identical MP4s. Short nine-frame CLI measurements and their client variability are reported separately below.

## Modifications

- Extend the existing ATen-compatible, vectorized-four Welford Triton kernel with FP32 modulation and FP32 affine epilogues. Normalize and modulate in FP32, then store BF16 once. Affine uses the native explicit FMA; modulation retains separate FP32 multiplication/addition. Existing BF16/FP8 defaults and `shift=None` behavior remain intact.
