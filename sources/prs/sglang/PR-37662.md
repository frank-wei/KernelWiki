## Motivation

Follow-up to #37480 (FastH3 with the VSA-H3 backend). This PR:

- moves native FastH3 support to the current checkpoint, [FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-8-Step-V2](https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-8-Step-V2), which replaces the 4-step preview;
- adds FastVideo's native Blackwell VSA kernel;
- speeds up the rest of the request path: denoise, VAE decode, and MP4 output. Encoding the MP4 while the VAE decodes is left to #41819.

The target is the workload from the [UniServe FastH3 post](https://haoailab.com/blogs/uniserve-fasth3/): 1344×768 at 24 fps with audio, 1K / 10K-token prompts, 5 / 10 / 15 s, latency measured from submission to the last byte of the MP4.

## Results: 4× GB300, FastH3 8-Step V2

Setup:
- Recipe: `sglang serve --model-path FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-8-Step-V2 --num-gpus 4 --component-attention-backends transformer=video_sparse_attn_h3`.
- Defaults (`quality: "extra-high"`, sparsity 0.8), BF16, eager, Ulysses 4, `NCCL_NVLS_ENABLE=0`.
- Workload: 72 requests at concurrency 1 after one warm request per shape. That is six scene families × {1K, 10K} tokens × {5, 10, 15} s × two seeds.
- Every prompt is unique, so no conditioning cache hits.
