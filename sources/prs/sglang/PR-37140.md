## Motivation

<!-- Describe the purpose and goals of this pull request. -->
`pack_fp8` is the fp8 quantization step in the DSv4, DSv3.2-indexer, MiniMax and Kimi-K3 kernels. On ROCm it is a hand-written bit twiddle, and it converts part of its input range wrong.

The overflow check is `exp8 >= kMaxExp`, and that is off by one. `exp8 == kMaxExp` is not overflow, it is the top exponent of the format, and e4m3 holds plenty of values there. So on gfx950 everything from 256 to 448 gets written out as 448. At the bottom of that range, that is 75% too big. Rounding is biased upward as well: ties go away from zero instead of to even, and one bf16 input in sixteen lands exactly on a tie.
