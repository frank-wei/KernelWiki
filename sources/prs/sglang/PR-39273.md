## Motivation

Serve the official GLM-5.3-Flash FP8 checkpoint and [`OneNexus/GLM-5.3-Flash-MXFP4`](https://huggingface.co/OneNexus/GLM-5.3-Flash-MXFP4) on MI355X (gfx950) at full speed and correct output. The target stack is zero-RoPE sparse attention, FP8 KV cache, and graph-enabled EAGLE 5/1/6.

`main` now includes all ten Day-0 enablement PRs (#38545, #38546, #38547, #39317, #39338, #39339, #39340, #39341, #39778, #39779). This PR adds the remaining correctness fixes and optimizations for serving Flash on gfx950. On the same four MI355X GPUs and AgentX replay, it measures **+14.3%** geomean output throughput over `main` at c1–c32, with higher P90 interactivity at every rung. At c48, `main` falls off the frontier, while this PR holds it.

The four CI and test-hygiene fixes that used to be here now live in #40721.

## What changed
