## Motivation

The exact Q/K head LayerNorm launch was tuned for H200 with 64 rows per Triton program. On RTX 5090, this configuration uses 142 registers per thread and limits the kernel to 25% theoretical / 23.11% achieved occupancy, making it materially slower than a smaller SM120 row group.

## Modifications

- Select `ROWS=8, num_warps=4` on SM120 and newer targets.
- Preserve the existing H100/H200 `ROWS=64, num_warps=2` launch unchanged.

## Accuracy Tests

- The fused kernel remains bit-exact against the eager per-head LayerNorm reference on RTX 5090.
- RTX 5090 changed Python-path tests: `14 passed` across Q/K LayerNorm, ERNIE, and Linear+GELU.
- H200 changed-path suite: `1265 passed`.
- H100 file-isolated diffusion suite: `3227 passed, 22 skipped`.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Production shape `(1, 4360, 32, 128)`, BF16 Q and K:

| GPU | Baseline (us) | Candidate (us) | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| RTX 5090 | 93.66 | 83.42 | -10.9% |
| H200 | 58.681 | 58.802 | +0.21% (noise) |
| H100 | 65.300 | 65.258 | -0.06% |

Nsight Compute on RTX 5090:

- Baseline: 142 registers/thread, 25% theoretical / 23.11% achieved occupancy.
- Candidate: 37 registers/thread, 100% theoretica
