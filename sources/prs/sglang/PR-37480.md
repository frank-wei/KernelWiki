## Motivation

Add [FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-4-step-Preview-v1-VSA-DataFree](https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastVideo-FastH3-4-step-Preview-v1-VSA-DataFree), the 4-step DMD2 distillation of MiniMax-H3 trained with Video Sparse Attention (0.9 sparsity, 64-token tiles), together with the `video_sparse_attn_h3` backend it was trained for.

4x B300, 1344x768 at 24 fps with audio, bf16 eager, Ulysses 4, `VSA_sparsity` 0.9, seed 1000. Clean E2E is the client wall clock of a `/v1/videos` request against `sglang serve` including decode, muxing and file output (median of three after one warm request); the stage columns are the server timings of the same request. H3 aligns the requested durations to 124, 243 and 362 frames.
