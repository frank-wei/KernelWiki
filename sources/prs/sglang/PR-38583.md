## Motivation

GLM-5.2 runs the DSA indexer in 78 layers (plus the MTP draft layer) on every decode step. At low concurrency on MI355X its short launches are a measurable part of inter-token latency. This PR adds a gfx950 decode path that does the whole indexer in four HIP kernels.

It is on by default where it is supported: gfx950, `SGLANG_USE_AITER=1`, aiter preshuffle, and GLM-5.2's indexer shape. `--no-enable-dsa-fused-indexer` turns it off.

## Modifications

Four kernels, header-only `.cuh`, JIT-built through `sglang.kernels.jit`:

| Kernel | What it does |
|---|---|
| `dual_gemv_bf16` | `q_lora @ wq_b.T` and `x @ [wk; weights_proj].T` in one launch |
| `qk_rope_hadamard_quant` | k LayerNorm, rope, 128-point Hadamard on q and k, fp8 quant of q, index-K cache write, head gate |
| `paged_mqa_logits` | fp8 paged MQA logits, plus the histogram the top-k needs |
| `topk_transform` | top-2048 and the page-table transform, split over 64 blocks per row using that histogram |
