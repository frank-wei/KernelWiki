## Motivation

Based on https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/32754 (gfx1250 enablement) and https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/36434 (ROCm 10 release images), see those PRs for the details of each.

On top of them, this adds the gfx1250-rocm1000 Docker target on the ROCm 10.0.0 GA wheel channel, gfx1250 detection in the AMD CI dependency installer, and the gfx1250 kernel/model fixes with the MI45x accuracy tests.
