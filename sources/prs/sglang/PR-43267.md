## Motivation

MiniMax-H3's AdaLN modulation (`indexed_scale_shift_bf16_`) and gated residual (`indexed_gate_bf16[_]`) Triton kernels launched one program per row with `BLOCK_N = next_power_of_2(hidden)`. For H3's 5376-wide rows that is 8192 lanes per program, and 37.5% of them are masked off. Each kernel runs twice per DiT block.

## Modifications

- Both kernels take a 2-D grid of rows × 2048-column tiles (`BLOCK_N = min(2048, next_power_of_2(hidden))`, 4 warps).
- The kernels are elementwise, so every output element is computed exactly as before.

## Accuracy Tests

`test/registered/kernels/ops/diffusion/test_indexed_modulation.py` (new) checks both kernels against the same arithmetic written in torch, byte for byte, including the in-place gate. It covers 1, 37 and 1025 rows at widths of one tile, a partial tile, whole tiles, and 5376. 24 passed on H200.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Per call on H200, 5376-wide rows; the row counts are H3's Ulysses 2 Ref2VA, Ulysses 2 T2VA and Ulysses 4 T2VA shards:
