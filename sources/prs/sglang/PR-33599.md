## Modifications

`python/sglang/kernels/ops/kimi_k3/attn_res_hip.py`:
one Triton kernel, one CTA per token. The bank is loaded as a single register-resident tile so scoring and mixing share one read of it, and the pending residual add, the bank snapshot and the output RMSNorm all fold into the same launch. Holding the tile is the trade — `MAX_REGISTER_TILE` caps the shape, because past that budget it spills and loses to the pipeline it replaces. K3 fits exactly at H=7168, `nvb <= 8`.
