## Motivation

MiniMax-H3 normalizes and rotates q/k with `fused_qknorm_rope_warp`. With head_dim 128 the kernel gives each warp one (token, head) row, so a lane moves only 8 bytes per row. The kernel is issue-bound, not bandwidth-bound: ncu shows 0.69 IPC per scheduler with 48% of stalls on L1TEX, and it reaches 2.5 TB/s on H200. It runs on every block: in place on the sequential Ulysses path, and once per head group and destination on the pipelined path (#43164).

## Modifications

- `fused_qknorm_rope_half_warp`: the same kernel with two rows per warp. Each row gets 16 lanes, and each lane moves 16 B of q/k and 16 B of the cos/sin cache.
- **Bit-identical by construction.** Each lane covers two lanes of the warp kernel. It keeps their two partial sums, accumulated in the same order, and replays that kernel's xor butterfly:
  - offsets 16..2 become 8..1, applied to each partial;
  - the final xor-1 step becomes `part0 + part1`.

  The per-element norm and RoPE expressions are unchanged.
- It is chosen at compile time for head_dim 128, NeoX, the norm rounded before RoPE, a half-width cache, and CUDA. Every other instantiation keeps the warp kernel.
