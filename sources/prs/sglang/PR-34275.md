## Summary

- let the fused QK-norm + RoPE CUDA path consume flattenable strided Q/K views
- fuse QK norm, RoPE, UND-prefix K/V copy, and GEN-suffix K/V packing into one JIT CUDA launch for Cosmos3 cross-attention
- retain the existing eager/SP/compiler fallbacks and cover packed GQA storage and multi-batch KV layout

## Why

Cosmos3-Nano policy generation runs 48 GEN layers per denoise step. Its Q/K tensors are views into one packed QKV projection, but the previous dispatch copied Q and K before the fused QK-norm + RoPE kernel. Cross-attention then launched two more `torch.cat` kernels per layer to assemble `[UND prefix | GEN suffix]` K/V.

The CUDA kernel already supports the packed QKV strides. This change uses those views directly and adds a KV-pack variant that writes normalized/rotated GEN K directly to its final attention buffer while copying prefix K and prefix/suffix V in the same launch. It does not use `torch.compile` or compiler fusion.
