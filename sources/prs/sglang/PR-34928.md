## Summary

- fuse Sana GLUMB convolution bias + SiLU and bias + GLU post-processing into bit-exact Triton kernels during BCG capture/replay
- reuse the shared bit-exact residual-gate-add kernel at the attention and MLP residual sites
- materialize the patch-token layout once instead of making every downstream LayerNorm copy the same transposed view
- verify each fused convolution path on first sight and permanently fall back to eager if the platform is not bit-exact

## Why

The B300 profile showed that Sana BCG was launch-bound after the existing LayerNorm fusion. Repeated `add`, `copy`, `mul`, activation, and gating operations around the GLUMB convolutions accounted for most of the remaining gap to `torch.compile`.

The new kernels preserve the BF16 materialization boundaries used by eager PyTorch, so `quality=lossless` output remains bit-exact.

## B300 performance

Measured in the 2026-08-14 full sweep from #21 on one NVIDIA B300, Sana 1.5 1.6B at the preset's default 1024px workload:
