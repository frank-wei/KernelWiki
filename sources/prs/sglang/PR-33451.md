## Motivation

FLUX.2 image generation spends 97.6 ms (1024^2) / 437.2 ms (2048^2) per image in VAE decode on H200. Profiling the decoder (diffusers `Decoder`) shows two structural problems:

- the `GroupNorm -> SiLU` chains upcast to fp32 under autocast and are ~46% of decode time — and they get *worse* if you naively switch to channels_last, because aten GroupNorm is ~2x slower on NHWC and the existing `triton_group_norm_silu` kernel forces a `.contiguous()` (NCHW) round-trip on channels_last inputs;
- the decoder runs NCHW, so every cuDNN conv pays nchwToNhwc/nhwcToNchw transpose kernels, and the D512 mid-block runs a single-head attention whose diffusers `AttnProcessor2_0` forward is layout-unsafe (calls `.view` on the 4D activation).

This PR adds (a) a layout-aware two-pass GroupNorm(+SiLU) Triton kernel and (b) a request-scoped, quality-gated FLUX.2 VAE decoder fast path built on it.
