## Summary
- Batch-invariant mHC mixing statistics for DeepSeek-V4.1's hyper-connection layers, in `mhc.py`: `hc_mix_stats` computes the per-row mixing projections and sum of squares over a fixed number of K slices with `tf32x3` dots, so a row's result is bitwise identical whether computed alone or in a batch; `hc_mix_stats_sinkhorn` fuses the slice reduction with the sinkhorn normalisation that `hc_split_sinkhorn` performs.
- Two compensated variants of the same projection for large batches, keeping the fp32 weight exact on the tensor cores: `hc_mix_stats_sinkhorn_bf16x3` (Triton, three bf16 weight components, one activation read) and `hc_mix_stats_sinkhorn_deepgemm` (tf32 high part plus fp32 residual through DeepGEMM), with `split_bf16_hc_weight` / `split_tf32_hc_weight` preparing the weight parts. Both accumulate over `_HC_MIX_COMPENSATED_SLICES` and share the sinkhorn reduce kernel; sizes come from the input shapes and `hc_mult`.
- `hc_split_sinkhorn` answers an empty batch directly instead of launching a zero-sized grid, which the TileLang backend rejects.
