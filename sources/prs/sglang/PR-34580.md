## Motivation

<!-- Describe the purpose and goals of this pull request. -->
The Triton MLA decode kernel picks its stage-1 geometry -- the number of KV splits and the block size along the KV axis -- from constants tuned on CDNA3. On gfx950 those constants fit badly at the batch sizes that matter for long-context serving. The split count is derived from a fixed workgroup budget, so at small batch a single split covers the whole sequence, while at large batch the budget is treated as a rounding target rather than a ceiling and stage-1 launches more workgroups than the device retires in one wave.
What the sweeps below isolate is the timing. The reading behind it -- CU underfill at small batch, one wave too many at large batch -- is the model these values were picked against, not something the A/B separates on its own.
