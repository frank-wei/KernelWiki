## Summary
- Native MXFP8 dense route for 32x32-block ue8m0 fp8 checkpoints on gfx950 (`ops/quantization/mxfp8_native_amd_gfx95.py`, `csrc/deepseek_v4/mxfp8_gemv_gfx95.cuh`): the weight is reordered once at load into the scaled-MFMA lane order and stays fp8, with K zero-padded to a multiple of 128 and the activation tail masked. M <= 32 runs a HIP GEMV on `v_mfma_scale_f32_16x16x128_f8f6f4`, larger M a Triton `tl.dot_scaled` GEMM. The input is bf16, bf16 on the fp8 grid, or fp8 + ue8m0 scales, and all three give the same bits.
- Tile tables (`mxfp8_gemv_gfx95_configs.json`) tuned at DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's TP4 shapes, fastest config per M bucket; `benchmark/kernels/quantization/tuning_mxfp8_native_gfx95.py` regenerates a shape's rows.
