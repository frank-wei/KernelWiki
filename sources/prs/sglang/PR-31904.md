## Motivation

KDA defines each per-channel decay gate in natural-log space. Let $g_t \in \mathbb{R}^{D_k}$ be the per-token log-decay gate and let its chunk-local cumulative gate be

$$
G_t = \sum_{s=1}^{t} g_s
$$

The decay that propagates information from position $j$ to position $i$ is

$$
D_{i,j} = \exp\left(G_i - G_j\right)
$$

The Triton chunk-prefill implementation mixed two exponent domains for the same cumulative-gate tensor:

- several intra-chunk kernels evaluated gate differences with `exp2`;
- the state-update, output, and fused-recompute paths evaluated them with natural `exp`;
- the cumulative sum remained in natural-log space.

$$
2^{G_i-G_j} = \exp\left(\ln(2)\left(G_i-G_j\right)\right)
$$
