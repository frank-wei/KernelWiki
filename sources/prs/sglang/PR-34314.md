## Motivation

After #34305 removes the per-forward FP8 weight dequant, Ideogram-4's remaining eager-vs-compile gap on H100 is its own elementwise soup (~15% of per-step GPU time in the TP=2 trace):

- the Qwen3-style rotate-half RoPE runs ~6 kernels per projection per layer (four muls, two add/subs, plus the sliced `empty_like` fills in `qwen3_apply_rotary_pos_emb`);
- the SwiGLU pair `F.silu(w1(x)) * w3(x)` makes two full passes over the FFN intermediate — the gate/up projections are separate GEMMs, so the concatenated-input `silu_and_mul` kernels would need an extra full-width `cat` pass that cancels the win.

## What this PR does

Both chains are pure elementwise, so their aten bf16 rounding boundaries can be reproduced exactly:

1. **RoPE** → one Triton kernel per projection (`rope_rotate_half_bitexact.py` — the same file #34306 adds for ERNIE; identical content, merges cleanly in either order). `round(round(q1·cos1) + round(−q2·sin1))` equals the eager `round(round(q1·cos1) − round(q2·sin1))` exactly under round-to-nearest.
