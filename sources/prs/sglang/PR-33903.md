## Motivation

Remove the helion dependency from the Inkling MoE activation path: port the two helion `silu_and_mul` kernels to plain Triton (bitwise-identical outputs), delete the helion AOT autotune machinery and its per-arch config tables, and drop `helion==1.4` from the package dependencies.

## Modifications

- Port the two helion `silu_and_mul` kernels in `python/sglang/kernels/ops/moe/inkling_moe.py` to plain Triton: `silu_and_mul_interleaved_kernel` and `silu_and_mul_non_interleaved_kernel` (public names, they show in profiler traces).
- Rename the wrapper `silu_and_mul_helion` to `silu_and_mul`. Update its one call site (`inkling_common/moe.py::activation`). The signature and behavior do not change.
- Delete the helion AOT autotune machinery: `srt/layers/moe/moe_runner/triton_utils/helion_utils.py` (its only importer was `inkling_moe.py`) and the seven `silu_and_mul{,_interleaved}_sm_{90,95,100,121}.json` configs. The new kernels are two static masked elementwise kernels with a small launch heuristic, so there is no per-arch config table to maintain.
- Drop the `helion==1.4` dependency from `python/pyproject.toml` and `python/pyproject_other.toml`. `import helion` is gone
<!-- upstream-excerpt-end -->
