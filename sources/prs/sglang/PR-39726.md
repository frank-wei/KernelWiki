## Motivation
The load-back half of the page-unified HiCache layout, and the mirror of the staged write-back added in #39606. That PR writes a device pool out into host pages ordered

  MHA: (page, head_group, layer, 2, page_size, head_in_group, dim), K=0 V=1
  MLA: (page, layer, page_size, dim)

but stops at write-back; nothing could read those pages back. This kernel does, for one layer at a time.

Per-layer and unstaged, unlike its write-back mirror. The two directions are not symmetric in how the runtime drives them: write-back moves a whole page at once, so staging it on the device and handing the bulk copy to the copy engine costs nothing extra. Load-back is walked layer by layer by the L2 transfer engine, which unblocks the forward pass one layer at a time, so staging whole pages per layer would move num_layers times the bytes the layer actually needs.
