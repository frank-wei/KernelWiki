## Motivation

With #41710 and an empty Triton cache, Wan2.2-TI2V-5B's first 720p request still decoded 0.17 s slower than later requests. Two costs remained in the VAE decode, and server warmup cannot absorb either of them:

- **Two DupUp3D-add variants compile during the decode.** #41710 unspecialized the sizes but kept every stride of `_dup_up3d_add_kernel`. In the channels-first arms those strides are products of the resolution (`C*H*W`, `W`, ...), so their divisibility class changes with the shape. Two launches landed in a class warmup had not compiled.
- **The lossless norm+SiLU fusion re-verifies itself at every new resolution.** `FusedWanRMSNormSiLU` keyed its first-sight exactness check on the activation's shape and strides. A new resolution therefore re-ran the eager reference chain plus a `torch.equal` host sync 46 times: 46 `cudaStreamSynchronize` calls in the first decode, none in the second.

Warmup cannot cover either: for TI2V, `InputValidationStage` derives the output size from the condition image's aspect ratio. The 64x64 warmup image gives 928x960, while the nightly's 1024x704 photo gives 1152x768, and no fixed warmup image covers every aspect
