## Motivation

Profiling Qwen-Image 2.1 (1024x1024, 40 steps, bf16, `--attention-backend torch_sdpa`) on one SM120 GPU shows each transformer block spending ~10.8 ms per step: 81% GEMM, 14% attention and ~5% everything else. That last slice was not bandwidth-limited work but data movement and launches around attention:

- two Triton kernels for Q/K RMSNorm + complex RoPE (`_qknorm_complex_rope_onepass_kernel`, 38 us; `_qknorm_complex_rope_kv_kernel`, 119 us), the second of which re-copies the full V projection (64 MiB per block-step) just to place it behind the text prefix;
- three separate Q/K/V GEMMs (N=4096 each) with their own tile tails;
- a single-element `torch.cat` on the attention output that degrades to a 33.5 MB device copy.

All three are removed here without changing a single output bit: every new path is default-on and verifies its first call against the eager chain through `BitExactFusionGate`, falling back permanently on a mismatch (the existing Qwen-Image 2.1 convention).
