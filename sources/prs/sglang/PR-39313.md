## Motivation
<!-- Describe the purpose and goals of this pull request. -->
Context: Deepseek-v4-pro uses DeepseekMoE (served as MegaMoE) as its mixure of experts with a shared expert that is activated across all tokens and a fine-grained routed experts.
- flash model: 1 shared + 256 routed
- pro model: 1 shared + 384 routed
>Note that the authors also chose to use FP4 with QAT such that routed expert weights are in MXFP4 while all other parameters (including the shared expert in the official checkpoints) stay in blockwise fp8 format.

For each token $t_i$ , a V4 MoE layer does two different GEMM operations:
1. shared - dense MLP on every token (in FP8)
2. routed - grouped GEMM over the top 6 selected experts (MXFP4)
