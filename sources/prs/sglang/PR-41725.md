## Motivation

Four changes on the GLM-5.2 decode path on gfx950, found by profiling a
GLM-5.2-MXFP4 TP8/EP1 server at 104k context. They are all the same shape: at
decode batch sizes these kernels leave a 256-CU part mostly idle, and none of
them is made faster by doing less arithmetic.

- two of them launch a handful of workgroups because the tile or the reduction
  was sized for a regime decode never enters;
- one spends more time in launch overhead than in work;
- one lands on torch because a tuned table keyed on `(N, K)` has no entry for
  the shape and does not fall back to a neighbour.

Together they are **+5.7%** end to end on that configuration.

## Modifications

**`moe`: fold the padded-row fill into the shared-expert append kernel.**
`_post_process_topk_ids` bracketed the aiter shared-expert append with two
separate fills over the padded region -- one for the routed ids, one for the
weights -- three launches for a shape whose total work is a couple of
microseco
