## Motivation

Recent additions have drifted from the logical operator groups in the [kernels RFC](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/29630): MiniCPM is a top-level group, Qwen PLE combines unrelated operators, attention modules own standalone GEMM/MoE operations, and quantization modules own matmul implementations. The corresponding tests also mix numerical kernels, runtime integration, and server evaluation.

This restores those boundaries, updates every in-tree caller, and documents the placement rules for future changes. The diff includes 178 byte-identical tuning-file moves.

## Modifications

- Group MiniCPM under attention; split Qwen PLE into embeddings, elementwise gates, and convolution-state operations. Rehome standalone DSV4 GEMM/MoE/normalization/quantization, Gemma routing, attention Top-K, Inkling projection/row operations, and hyperconnection matmul into their owning groups. Keep fused attention operations with attention.
- Separate FP8/INT8 GEMM from quantization and move its tuning files together, preserving relative config lookup. Update runtime, benchmark, test, and lazy registry references.
- Put active K3 communication adapters under `srt/layers/comm
