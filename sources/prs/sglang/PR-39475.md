## Motivation

<!-- Describe the purpose and goals of this pull request. -->
The Cosmos3 Nano simulation family consists of four NVIDIA world models for robotics and
autonomous driving. They share the Cosmos3 Omni weights, Wan VAE and tokenizer with the
already-supported `Cosmos3Pipeline`, but each needs a rollout the single-pass bidirectional
pipeline cannot express:

- **Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Bimanual**: image + prompt + per-frame robot or camera actions to video,
  generated causally in chunks by a distilled 4-step SDE student, with clean K/V committed as
  history. Long rollouts need a bounded history (deploy-time window and sinks, camera-frustum
  K/V retrieval) and realtime tick sessions driven by actions as they arrive.
- **Cosmos3-Nano-Sim-Transfer**: the same causal recipe conditioned on a control video
  (depth, segmentation, edge, blur) instead of actions, with the newer direct-resize
  preprocessing its latest checkpoin
