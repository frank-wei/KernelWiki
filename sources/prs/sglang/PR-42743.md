## Motivation

Add native Kandinsky 6.0 audio/video generation and distilled video super-resolution to SGLang-Diffusion.

Opening as a draft for public review. The implementation has real GPU and HTTP coverage, but official-reference alignment and standard model CI baselines are not complete.

## Modifications

- Native Pro / Pro-distill text- and image-conditioned joint video/audio generation, Reason1 and CLIP conditioning, PiFlow scheduling, and audio/video decoding. No external model pipeline at runtime.
- Native distilled SR pipeline with video uploads, audio preservation, x2/x4/fractional scaling, latent upsampling, and tiled KVAE decoding.
- TP, Ulysses, Ring, CFG parallelism, FSDP, component/layerwise offload, LoRA and request-level Cache-DiT integration. Support and numerical limits are documented per pipeline; this is not a claim that every combination is validated.
- Matrix-RoPE and FP32 residual-gate kernels, plus shared fixes required by this model: exact LoRA base-weight restoration, CFG-local cache decisions, batched Ring attention, and PCIe-safe IPC selection.
