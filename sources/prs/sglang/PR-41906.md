## Motivation

Each block of the H3 video VAE decoder runs eager RMSNorm, a scaled residual add, and per-head Q/K RMSNorm plus partial NeoX RoPE. The decoder is a 36-block ViT and runs on every H3 request. Decode is still one tile per call.

## Modifications

- **`h3_vae_rmsnorm`**, **`h3_vae_scale_add_rmsnorm`**, **`h3_vae_qk_rmsnorm_rope`** (`kernels/ops/diffusion/norm/h3_vae_fused_norm.py`): FP32 RMSNorm, the scaled residual add fused into the next RMSNorm, and affine-free Q/K RMSNorm plus partial NeoX RoPE (head 64 / rotary 48). A contract mismatch stays on the eager path. `MINIMAX_H3_VAE_DECODER_FUSED_NORM=1` by default.
- The decoder broadcasts one rotary table across the tiles in a batch, so the Q/K kernel also runs for `B > 1`.

## Accuracy

Fused matches eager to a max absolute error of about 5e-5 on a 28-tile output. `test_h3_vae_fused_norm.py` checks each kernel. `test_fused_decoder_matches_unfused_forward` checks the decoder and that the fused kernels actually ran.

## Speed

One H200, fp16 autocast, released decoder shape, one tile per call. A 1344×768, 10 s, 24 fps clip is 14 temporal units, 28 tiles each. The unit row is measured. The 10 s row is that unit times 14:
