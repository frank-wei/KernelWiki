## Motivation

PyTorch eager `argmax` over 129k-151k draft logits has limited parallelism at small decode batch sizes, and the `topk=1` chain path still materializes intermediate tensors and Python lists.

This is part 1 of 3 extracted from #30388 in response to the review request to split the three optimization areas.

## Changes

- Add a split-reduction Triton `draft_topk1_postprocess` kernel under the canonical `sglang.kernels.ops.speculative` namespace.
- Register `speculative.draft_topk1_postprocess` as a Triton `KernelSpec`.
- Fuse position advancement and, on CUDA, the draft-token column store.
- Special-case the `topk=1` chain in `EagleWorkerV2.draft_forward` to avoid `select_top_k_tokens`, per-step token lists, and the final `torch.cat`.
- Preserve rejection-sampling and non-CUDA fallback behavior.
- Add focused kernel, registry, and benchmark coverage.
- Remove two stale imports of the nonexistent `sglang.srt.speculative.triton_ops.eagle` module.

## Impact

This removes repeated draft-loop launches and intermediate materialization from the common `topk=1` EAGLE/MTP path.
