## Motivation

`amd/Qwen3.5-397B-A17B-MXFP4-AttnFP8-V2` keeps the attention and GDN projections in FP8, with per-channel weights and dynamic per-token activations. At decode sizes on MI355X TP4, each layer runs two quant + GEMM pairs through `gemm_a8w8_bpreshuffle`.

The GEMMs are the first problem. They read their weights far below the bandwidth a plain streaming read reaches. At 4 tokens:

- packed GDN `in_proj` (21 MB): 10.0 us, versus 4.8 us for a streaming read of the same size
- `qkv_proj` (19 MB): 9.7 us, versus 4.5 us
- `out_proj` / `o_proj` (8 MB): 5.4 us, versus 2.8 us

The second problem is the output-side activation quant, after the GDN gated norm and the attention gate multiply. It runs as its own launch.

This PR adds a small-M GEMM for these shapes and folds the output-side quant into the kernel that produces the activation.
