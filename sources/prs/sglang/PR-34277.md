## Motivation

Follow-up to #27926: DSV4 still emits FP32 activation scales, so DeepGEMM runs `transpose_and_pack_fp32_into_ue8m0` before every `fp8_einsum` (61 launches, ~114 µs per BS1 decode step).

## Modification

Emit DeepGEMM's TMA-aligned packed UE8M0 `int32` scale layout directly from the DSV4 quant kernel. Quantized outputs and einsum numerics are unchanged.

## Speedup

DeepSeek-V4-Pro, TP8 on 8×B200, ISL 16 / OSL 1024. Values are medians over 1,023 decode steps.

| BS | Main latency (ms) | This PR (ms) | Saved (µs) | Throughput gain |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 10.5033 | 10.3778 | 125.5 | 1.21% |
| 2 | 10.7202 | 10.6135 | 106.7 | 1.01% |
| 4 | 10.8403 | 10.7365 | 103.8 | 0.97% |
| 8 | 11.5747 | 11.5213 | 53.4 | 0.46% |
| 16 | 13.3792 | 13.0686 | 310.6 | 2.38% |
| 32 | 16.0405 | 15.4845 | 556.0 | 3.59% |
| 64 | 19.6261 | 19.5289 | 97.2 | 0.50% |

Geometric mean: **1.42% lower decode latency / 1.44% higher throughput**.
