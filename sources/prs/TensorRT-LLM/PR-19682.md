## Description

At small token counts the trtllm-gen custom routing (`routingIndicesBlockKernel`, 1 CTA) spends most of its time in serialization around a ~1.6 us TopK: an init sweep of two `[8 x 256]` tables, a barrier, the PDL wait, a warp-per-token TopK whose post-processing reloads the selected experts' bias from global memory, a barrier, a per-expert histogram, two `cub::BlockScan`s, tile maps and the permutation loop, ~10 barriers in total. On GB300 at 6 tokens (GLM-5.2, TP8, bs1, MTP depth 5) it measures 5.6 us standalone and adds ~5.4 us/layer behind the router GEMM under CUDA graphs + PDL, x75 MoE layers = ~0.4 ms of a ~9 ms decode step.

This PR adds `routingIndicesSmallTokenKernel` (new TU `RoutingCustomSmallToken.cu`) for `numTokens <= min(8, warps of the tier)` and expert tiers `<= 512`:
