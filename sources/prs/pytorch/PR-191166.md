## Summary

Adds gfx1250 Tensor Data Mover (TDM) descriptor support to TorchInductor for dense `mm` and `addmm`, together with the shared capability foundation that two follow-up PRs consume.

This is the first of three pieces split out of the original combined change, which was 2,121 lines and over the `pr-sanity-checks` 2,000-line limit. This piece is **1,429 changed lines**.

| Piece | Content | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **This PR** | shared foundation + dense `mm` / `addmm` | open, 1,429 lines |
| Generic descriptor codegen | pointwise / reduction tensor-descriptor path | branch [`tdm/split-pr2`](https://github.com/glen-amd/pytorch/tree/tdm/split-pr2); opens after this lands, 242 lines |
| Flex attention forward / decode | `USE_TMA` descriptor path | branch [`tdm/split-pr3`](https://github.com/glen-amd/pytorch/tree/tdm/split-pr3); opens after this lands, 446 lines |

The three are siblings, not a chain: each consumes the foundation and none consumes another. The follow-ups stay unopened because a `pytorch/pytorch` PR cannot be based on a fork-only branch; once this lands they rebase onto `main` independently and their diffs shrink to the sizes above.
