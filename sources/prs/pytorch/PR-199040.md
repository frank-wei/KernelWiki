## Agent note

> The default ranked SM100 search omitted existing small swapped tiles that help skinny dense GEMMs. Add two legal swapped 128x32 candidates for tuned plain mm with M <= 128, using shape hints from lowering. This normally expands the search from 12 to 14 candidates.
>
> Keep larger-M, grouped, block-scaled, and untuned selection unchanged. A global search expansion was rejected because it regressed larger nonlinear workloads in the measured cases.
>
> Tests cover the M cutoff and grouped exclusion, and exercise both candidates through production search and legality checks with BF16 and FP16 SiLU.
