AI-assisted content:

> ## Problem
>
> Fixes #198174
> Fixes #198175
>
> NVGEMM kernels could not participate in CUDA programmatic dependent launch (PDL), so direct Triton consumers waited for ordinary kernel completion instead of overlapping their launch setup with the producer tail.
>
> ## Summary
>
> Add selective PDL for measured NVFP4 decode shapes and connect eligible NVGEMM providers to direct same-stream Triton consumers.
>
> ## Architecture
>
> The NVGEMM heuristics module owns the complete shape policy. In `"auto"` mode it generates PDL-capable operators only for NVFP4 1x16 problems with `0 < M <= 256` and logical `N` and `K` in `[1024, 65536]`, excluding the measured regression band `32 < M <= 64` and `2048 < min(N, K) < 4096`. `"0"` disables PDL, while `"1"` forces it for experiments. The policy receives packed FP4 K from `MMKernelInputs` and performs the packed-to-logical conversion internally.
>
> The selected policy is represented in operator metadata and included in generated-pre
