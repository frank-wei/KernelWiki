> raw throw in a `.cc`, `.cu`, `.cuh`, `.hpp` or `.mm` file was invisible to
> it. Emptying the exclude list without fixing that would leave the check
> narrower than it looks, which is why this lands before the last two PRs of
> the stack rather than after them.
>

> entry.** Widening `include_patterns` is the equivalent strengthening move:
