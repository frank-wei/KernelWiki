> FSDP2 copies nonzero-dimension shards through intermediate buffers on both
> sides of its collectives: the all-gather copy-out splits the rank-major
> output by rank and concatenates the pieces along the shard dim, and the
> reduce-scatter copy-in chunks each gradient and concatenates the chunks by
> destination rank. This adds two private ops that copy directly between a
> collective buffer and the parameter or gradient layout,
> `fsdp::_all_gather_copy_out_(out, input, split_sizes, outer_sizes, num_chunks)`
> and `fsdp::_reduce_scatter_copy_in_(out, tensors, num_leading_dims, num_chunks)`.
> Nothing in FSDP2 calls them yet; #197701 adds the opt-in that does.
>
> The CUDA kernels live in ChunkCat.cu and reuse its chunk-cat kernel, which
> `chunk_cat_mixed_dtype` now shares: the reduce-scatter copy-in treats each
> index into a gradient's leading dims as one chunk-cat input and casts bf16
> or fp16 into an fp32 b
