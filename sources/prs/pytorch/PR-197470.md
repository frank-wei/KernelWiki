## Summary (human written only)

`torch.sort` and `torch.topk` reject dimensions longer than INT_MAX on CUDA and ROCm (on ROCm this also breaks fp16/bf16 `topk` above 2^32). This removes that ceiling on the sort path, where CUB/rocPRIM already take a 64-bit size, fixes the two 32-bit computations underneath it, and stops ROCm routing such slices to the full-sort fallback. Other `radix_sort_pairs` callers keep the check. Two slow regression tests at n = 2^31 + 2 pass on MI350 and run in the MI350 slow job; at 60/80 GB they don't run on CUDA CI. Details and perf discussion are in the review thread.
