## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Replace `data_ptr<T>()` with `mutable_data_ptr<T>()` where the buffer is written, in nested tensor kernels, to make write intent explicit. No behavior change is intended.

Split by area from a larger change.

> Addressed review: bmm_nested_cuda's aptr/bptr fed read-only self/mat2
> buffers through mutable_data_ptr; reverted to data_ptr since they are
> only read. Also fixed a missed data_ptr -> const_data_ptr on a
> read-only offsets buffer in NestedTensorBinaryOps.cu.

This PR was authored with an AI assistant (Claude Code).
