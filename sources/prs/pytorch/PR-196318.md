### Agent Notes

> Add the variable-length-M, 2-D/3-D form of `torch.nn.functional.grouped_mm` to FlexGEMM:
> BF16/FP16 `A [total_m, K]`, `B [E, K, N]` (transposed weights), int32 end offsets `offs [E]`,
> and output `[total_m, N]`. Ragged lengths and empty groups are supported. This includes the
> grouped GEMMs used by MoE expert layers, without making the API specific to that application.
>
> The HOP moves `offs` from `gemm_kwargs` into tensor operands, avoiding tensor constant-folding.
> Inductor resolves the native schema, constructs `cu_seqlens_m = constant_pad_nd(offs, [1, 0])`
> in the graph, and passes it to QuACK's existing varlen-M EpiMod path. Bias, `out_dtype`, 3-D A,
> the 2-D/2-D weight-gradient form, and scaled grouped-MM remain explicitly unsupported.
>
