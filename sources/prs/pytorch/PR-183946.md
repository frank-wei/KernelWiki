The CUDA implementation for native_group_norm promotes (b)float16 inputs to float32 during computation, for accuracy.  However, we previously truncated the calculated mean and variance back to (b)float16 mid-computation.  This commit postpones truncation until the end of the kernel, increasing accuracy (and removing differences with the decomposed kernel in Inductor).

Additionally, I enabled clang-format linting for this file, which changed less than 10 lines and seemed good to enable while I was in it.
