* __->__ #198789

**Problem.** A model also launches Triton kernels that Inductor never compiles (`@triton.jit` / `@triton.autotune` functions, for example in fbgemm or custom ops), and in a fresh serving process each of them is JIT-compiled and autotuned on first call, which is serve-time compilation that no precompile cache entry covers and that `no_compilation()` refuses.

**Fix.** `precompile.capture()` gives Triton a fresh private cache directory with its autotuning cache on for the life of its runtime scope and exports that directory into the precompile cache as a `triton_runtime` artifact at every write, and `precompile.load()` imports it, before any graph artifact, as part of loading the cache.

**Summary.** Before this change the precompile cache carried only what Inductor compiled (the frozen static Inductor Triton kernels and the frozen C++ kernels), so kernels launched directly through Triton's JIT, together with their autotuning decisions, were compiled and benchmarked again on every serving rank.
