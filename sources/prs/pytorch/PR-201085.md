### Human Note

Fast math is my fun max perf land; this is for that, we only do the substitution when that is set, has big wins for sitglu bwd

## Agent note

> CuTeDSL epilogues lowered every `x / c` to a full-precision division, which is an instruction sequence (reciprocal plus refinement FMAs) rather than one op, even with `fast_math`. In math-heavy FlexGEMM epilogues this dominated: a SiTU-GLU backward fused into its dgrad GEMM (T=8192, D=2048, I=1408, E=8) took 163.5 us against a 55.6 us identity GEMM; writing the same `gate / 4.0`, `up / 25.0` as multiplies brought it to 83.8 us, and the full MoE fwd+bwd step went from 605 to 457 us.
>
> Root cause: `CuteDSLOpOverrides.truediv` emitted `a / b` unconditionally. It now emits `a * (1 / c)` for a finite nonzero constant divisor `c`: always when `c` is a power of two (the reciprocal is exact, so the result is bit-identical), and for other constants only under `fast_math` (may differ by one ulp). Tensor divisors and division by zero keep the existing lowering
