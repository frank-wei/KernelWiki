## Linked issue or supporting maintainer

Supported by @jeffdaily

Implements the ROCm Triton `reduce` (previously a `static_assert` stub) on top of rocSHMEM's device reduce, `rocshmem_{short,int,long,float,double}_{sum,min,max,prod}_reduce_wg`. It then enables the reduce tests in `test_shmem_triton.py` on ROCm.

Changes:
- `_rocshmem_triton.py`: `reduce` maps int16/int32/int64/float32/float64 and sum/min/max/prod to the rocSHMEM wrappers. `team` and `nreduce` are passed as int32, because rocSHMEM takes C `int` for both.
- The rocSHMEM wrappers only accept team 0 (all PEs). A compile-time team other than 0 raises `ValueError`. A runtime team other than 0 does nothing and returns non-zero. The docstring states this.
- The rocSHMEM minimum version goes from 3.3.0 to 3.5.0 (ROCm 7.14). 3.5.0 is the first release that exports these wrappers. Updated in `caffe2/CMakeLists.txt`, `rocshmem_extension.cu`, and the `_symmetric_memory/__init__.py` docstring. ROCm 10.0 (3.6.0) and 10.1 (3.7.0) both meet it.
- Tests: removed `@skip_if_rocm_multiprocess` from `test_triton_{sum,minmax,prod}_reduce`. rocSHMEM has no int8/uint8/float16/bfloat16 reduce, so those dtypes skip on ROCm. float64 sum/prod
