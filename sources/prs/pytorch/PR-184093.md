* __->__ #184093

Inductor CSE can emit floating-point self-subtraction such as tmp - tmp when two duplicate producers are deduplicated. With Triton fp fusion enabled, LLVM can reassociate through the producer and lower the expression as an FMA, so finite values that should subtract to exact zero can produce tiny nonzero results.

Rewrite only floating-point CSE self-subtraction at Triton codegen time. Finite tmp - tmp is emitted as zero, while nan and inf inputs still take the non-finite path and produce nan, preserving subtraction semantics without disabling fp fusion for the entire kernel. This replaces the broader per-kernel fp-fusion disable that was considered first.

Fixes #154150
