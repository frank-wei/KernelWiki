## Problem

Per-subkernel-block combo kernels currently inline every member body into one merged Triton function. ptxas then performs register allocation over the combined control-flow graph, so one register-heavy member can inflate the whole combo binary's register allocation and reduce occupancy for every member.

On the Qwen3-0.6B QK-norm pair (B200, CUDA 12.8), two members that compile standalone to 128 registers / 12 spills each become a 151-register combo binary when their bodies are inlined. Restructuring the dispatch does not help; the joint allocation is inherent to the single-body form.

## Fix

Emit each per-subkernel-block member body as a `@triton.jit(noinline=True)` device function and call it from the combo dispatch branch. Each member now codegens in its own local `KernelArgs` namespace, so noinline bodies use canonical local names such as `in_ptr0`, `out_ptr0`, and `XBLOCK`.
