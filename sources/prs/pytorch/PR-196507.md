Human Notes:

Extend the AOT toolchains to include triton - in this case we get a .cubin from
triton and embed in a generated CUDA driver wrapper.

Claude:

TritonToolchain compiles to a raw cubin and generates its own launcher: cubin
bytes embedded in the .cpp, per-device call_once cuModuleLoadData, grid exprs from
the builder's launch spec. Deliberately not triton.tools.compile's .c template --
that template calls the driver directly and, being triton-generated, cannot be
routed through torch's dlopen wrapper, so linking it would put a libcuda NEEDED on
torch_cuda and break CUDA builds on driver-less machines. Every driver call
therefore goes through ATen's NVRTC table, as every other cubin launcher in the
tree does (jiterator, nativert, inductor's static launcher), which also buys
per-device module load and AT_CUDA_DRIVER_CHECK over the template's
current-context-only load and exit()-on-error. read_only args use const_data_ptr.
