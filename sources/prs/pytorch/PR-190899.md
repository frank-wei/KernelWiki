Human Notes:

Finally do a thing! Add `topk` as an AOT-provided override in libtorch_cuda.

Claude:

Declares a 48-point grid (fp32/bf16 x N{2048..16384} x K{64,128,256}
x both determinism modes) over the radix-select kernel. The det
specialization (stable prefix-sum gather + lex (ord, -idx) sort) is
bit-exact vs aten including tie resolution, so det-mode calls are
served AOT too; the dispatch chain keys on
deterministicAlgorithms(). The kernel gains
in_dtype and index_dtype compile parameters; the AOT route writes
int64 indices directly (no widening copy). build(spec) lives beside
the JIT wrappers, so both routes compile the same class. fp16 and
off-grid shapes keep their JIT eligibility, as do calls the prelude
declines: a non-last dim, largest/sorted false, sub-full-wave M, or a
base pointer less aligned than the kernels were compiled for.

ARCHS is stated rather than defaulted, and covers Hopper as well as
Blackwell: measured on an H100, the AOT kernels beat aten on 48/48 of
the grid, median 2.16x (min 1.26x at M=4096/N=2048, max 2.99x at
M=256/N=4096 fp
