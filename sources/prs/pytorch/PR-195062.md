Cut over the existing inner-tree implementations to the new stack.

Codex:

Route the existing ordered `aten::sum` and `aten::prod` overrides through the
shared fixed-DAG kernel. Eligibility, TensorIterator canonicalization,
keepdim, and direct/out overload handling remain in the adapter. Unsupported
dtypes and geometry continue to fall through to ATen.

the plan, with underaligned rows selecting the bit-neutral unstaged path.
