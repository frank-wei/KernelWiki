> Wire scheduler Loop IR epilogue plans into NVGEMM fusion and CuTeDSL code
> generation. When the scheduler considers another node, NVGEMM reinterprets
> the complete candidate prefix, validates that every reduction is claimed, and
> accepts the node only when the resulting program has a supported lowering.
>
> `LoopIRCuteDSLCodegen` replays the captured expressions through CuTeDSL
> operation overrides to generate the tensor epilogue and any source, combine,
> finalize, or consumer callbacks. Dense and block-scaled providers consume the
> same generated contract.
>
> Reduction selection is based on captured IR and geometry, not an algorithm
> name. The reduction kind identifies only the associative primitive. A
> fragment-local composition can contain multiple reductions and pointwise
> operations and returns its local result from the generated epilogue. Geometry
> that requires cross-thread work receives generated combine and finalize
>
