# FP component housekeeping ahead of the `cuda::` promotion

Nine independent cleanups to the FP component (`<cuda/fpmp>`, `<cuda/fpemu>`,
`<cuda/fptool>`), collected while the component is still in `cuda::experimental`
and the namespace contents are cheap to change. Each commit stands alone and is
reviewable on its own; the list below is in apply order.

### Namespace and macro hygiene
- **Unify the fpemu guard-bit count on `_CCCL_FPEMU_EXTRA_BITS`.** The count of 9
  was spelled three ways, one of them a bare `EXTRA_BITS` that `<cuda/fpemu>`
  leaked into the translation unit and that an `#ifndef` let a user silently
  redefine, changing the emulated arithmetic.
- **Keep libm out of the component's namespace, and `::`-qualify every global
  call.** `sqrt`, `sqrtf` and `fma` were declared *inside* the namespace, so C
  linkage made them aliases of libm; after the promotion `cuda::sqrt(2.0)` would
  have reached libm and any later C++-linkage `cuda::sqrt` would have been a hard
  conflict.
