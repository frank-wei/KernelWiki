## Description

The remaining `cuda_safe_call` sites outside the files already covered by #11740, #11748, #11770, #11772 and #11773: twenty-two sites across eight headers, grouped by what the call does rather than where it lives.

- `shared_ptr` deleters destroying a `cudaGraphExec_t`: `algorithm.cuh` (two), `executable_graph_cache.cuh`.
- `SCOPE(fail)` releasing a graph that is still ours: `stackable_ctx_impl.cuh` (two).
