## Summary
- migrate throwing-capable CUDA calls in `graph_task.cuh` from `cuda_safe_call` to `cuda_try`
- keep `cuda_safe_call` in `SCOPE(fail)` capture-abort paths (noexcept)
- end stream capture on exception after `BeginCapture` / `BeginCaptureToGraph` so streams are not left capturing
- use runtime-status `cuda_try(...)` for multi-output `cudaStreamGetCaptureInfo*`
- simplify `record_time` predicates
