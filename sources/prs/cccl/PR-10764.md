## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
Not every SM of a device necessarily participates in a strict per-domain SM split: SMs may sit outside any locality domain, and the default co-scheduling alignment can leave a domain's incomplete SM groups unassigned. Introduce locality_domain_sm_split so execution-place construction chooses how the per-domain partitions are carved out of the device with cuDevSmResourceSplit:

- backfill (the new default): every domain place is an even share of the device total, backfilled by the driver (target domain first, then SMs outside any domain, then other domains), so the domain places together cover the whole device. Backfilled SMs may lack memory affinity with the place's domain, and the finest co-scheduling granularity forfeits thread-block cluster launches.
- aligned (the previous behavior): only complete co-scheduled groups of the domain at the device's default alignment; strictly domain-affine and cluster-capable, but incomplete groups and undomained SMs are left out.
- fine: all of the domain's SMs at the finest co-scheduling granularity; strictly domain-affine, at the cost of cluster launches.
