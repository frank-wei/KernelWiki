## Description

Suggested by @Jacobfaib in the clang-tidy-for-STF discussion: replace plain `throw` with `_CCCL_THROW` throughout the STF and places headers.

- **75 sites** converted from `throw T(args);` to `_CCCL_THROW(T, args);`, plus one bare rethrow to `_CCCL_RETHROW`, across `__stf` and `__places`; `exception_macros.h` included directly where it was not already.
- **Deliberately not converted**: throws inside `UNITTEST` blocks — they are the subject under test for `on_throw` and the expecter — and prose mentions in comments.

`_CCCL_THROW` is the right idiom for header code preprocessed in both compilation passes: a real `throw` on the host, `terminate()` on the device. Beyond consistency, the device-pass AST stops carrying throw statements that could never execute there, which makes static analysis of the device pass truthful about CCCL-owned throw sites.

**Second commit, also per @Jacobfaib**: the context/task payload variants (and their accessors) now come from `cuda::std` instead of `std`, whose `bad_variant_access` throw is visible inline in libstdc++ headers.
