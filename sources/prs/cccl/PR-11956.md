## Description

Moves the FP component (`fpmp`, `fpemu`, `fptool`) from `cuda::experimental` into `cuda`.

* Namespace promotion:
   - The headers in `libcudacxx/include/cuda/__fp/` now open `_CCCL_BEGIN_NAMESPACE_CUDA`.
   - Their internal references are qualified with `::cuda::`.
   - Their banners now say libcu++.
   - Tests, examples (`examples/cudax/fp`) and docs (`docs/libcudacxx/extended_api/fp*.rst`) are updated to `cuda::`.
   - The generated `fpmp_spec.rst` is regenerated.
+ **Qualify internal routine calls with `::cuda::`**
   - Calls to the component's own functions no longer go through argument-dependent lookup.
   - The change is mechanical: declarations, class members and CUDA intrinsics stay unqualified.

Verification:
- The instantiated-function symbol sets for all 54 fp tests are identical before and after the qualification commit, on both host and device.
- The fp tests pass on host, device and NVRTC, in C++17 and C++20.
- Every public `<cuda/...>` and `<cuda/std/...>` header still compiles when included after the fp headers.
- The docs build with Sphinx `-W`.
