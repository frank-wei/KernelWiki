## Description

Drops the STF exclusion from `ci/build_tidy.sh` so the clang-tidy job runs over CUDA STF, and works through what the first run reported: 1237 findings across 214 TUs. A second run after the first two batches reported 58; the third batch addresses those.

**Batch 1, by script from the reported locations (1097 sites):** `misc-const-correctness` (690), `bugprone-narrowing-conversions` (280, explicit `static_cast` of the operand), `cert-err34-c` (92, `atoi`/`atol`/`atof` to `std::sto*`), `clang-diagnostic-braced-scalar-init` (25), `modernize-deprecated-headers` (10). The negative-compile TUs under `test/stf/static_error_checks` are marked `NO_CLANG_TIDY`.

**Batch 2, by hand (~110 sites):** range-for conversions, `reserve()` before push loops, `rand()` replaced by `<random>`, integer-division and nested-`min` cleanups, `emplace_back`, `noexcept` moves (conditional on the wrapped callable for the scope-guard results), `= default`, a self-assignment guard, `scoped_lock`, `fseek` for `rewind`, `using` for `typedef`, matching override visibility, plus suppressions with a stated reason where the finding is the point of the code.
