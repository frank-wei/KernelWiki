Addresses #9587 (Device-level Reduce/Scan/Sort part).

Extends the benchmark type axes to include `__half` and `__nv_bfloat16` for:

- `DeviceReduce` sum (`cuda::std::plus<>`) and min (`cuda::minimum<>`)
- `DeviceScan` exclusive sum (both the classic and lookahead variants)
- `DeviceRadixSort` `SortKeys` and `SortPairs` (key axis)

To make this work, `nvbench_helper` now instantiates its uniform data generators (`gen_device`/`gen_host`) for the extended FP types:

- `random_to_item_t` classifies types with `cuda::is_floating_point_v` instead of `std::is_floating_point_v`, so `__half`/`__nv_bfloat16` take the floating-point path.
- `and_t` gains bitwise overloads for `__half`/`__nv_bfloat16` (used by the entropy-reduction path).
- The `INSTANTIATE` macro is split so the extended FP types instantiate only the uniform generators; the segmented-sort key generators are left out until segmented benchmarks need them.
