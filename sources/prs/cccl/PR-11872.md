## Upstream PR description (verbatim excerpt)

<!-- upstream-excerpt-start -->
`CompareDeviceResults` read results back with a blocking `cudaMemcpy` on the legacy default stream, while the tests and examples allocate and free their buffers stream-ordered (`cuda::make_buffer`/`make_device_buffer`) on a custom stream. compute-sanitizer memcheck reports this as a use-after-free in `cub.test.block.radix_rank`.

This adds a `cuda::stream_ref` parameter to `CompareDeviceResults` (defaulting to the default stream), copies with `cudaMemcpyAsync` on it and synchronizes it, and passes the stream at the call sites that use stream-ordered buffers.
